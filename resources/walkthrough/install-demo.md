# Explore a Full Demo World

Running **Install Demo World...** downloads a complete, finished project
straight from GitHub — full seasons of scripts, dozens of entities, a
Story Overview, canon-status tracking already in use — and opens it in a
new window. Nothing in your current workspace is touched.

Two demos are available:

- **Of One Blood** — a literary adaptation of Pauline Hopkins' 1902 novel.
- **Loomwake** — a GM-focused sci-fi TTRPG campaign, deliberately left
  unfinished for you to continue.
