Professor Stone's and, independently, Ai's shared historical argument — that Ethiopia and Meroe, not Egypt, were the true origin point of civilization's arts, sciences, and government, and that the Biblical Cush/Ham genealogy ties the "Chaldean" and Babylonian civilizations directly to Africa. Hopkins gives this real narrative weight: Professor Stone lectures on it at length in camp, and Ai independently confirms it from Telassar's own records, framing the hidden city not as a fantasy but as the last living proof of a real, buried history.
