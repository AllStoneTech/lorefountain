James Vance's family home in Mount Auburn, near Cambridge, across the grounds from [[Hyde House]]. [[Dianthe Lusk]] is taken here to recover after the train wreck and lives here under the name "Felice Adams" through her courtship with [[Reuel Briggs]]; it's also where the Hallow-eve dare that first brings Reuel face to face with her begins.
