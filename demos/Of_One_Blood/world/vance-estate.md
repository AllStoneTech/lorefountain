James Vance's family home in Mount Auburn, near Cambridge, across the grounds from [[Hyde House]]. [[Dianthe Lusk]] is brought here from the hospital, months after the train wreck, at Molly's insistence, and lives here under the name "Felice Adams" through her courtship with [[Reuel Briggs]]; it's also where the Hallow-eve dare that first brings Reuel before her vision begins.
