# Where the novel contradicts itself

Checked chapter by chapter against the Project Gutenberg text (`imports/of-one-blood.md`). None of these is introduced by this adaptation; each is left as the novel has it, with the entities involved marked `canon_status: contradicted` where an entity is at stake.

1. **Laurel Hill, Virginia vs. Livingston Hall, Maryland.** Chapter VII has Aubrey plan to "remove to Virginia and renew the ancient splendor of his ancestral home"; his telegram (VIII) and Jim Titus's letter (XI) use "Laurel Hill, Virginia." Chapter XX places "Livingston's ancestral home in Maryland" (Livingston Hall; "Livingston Place" in XXI), and Aunt Hannah lives within walking distance of it. See `livingston-hall.md` and `livingston-plantation.md`.
2. **Ababdis vs. Abdallah.** The camel-owner is Ababdis in Chapters X, XIII, XV and XXIV and "Abdallah" five times in XVIII–XIX; "Abdallah" is also the Tripoli sheik's name (X). See `ababdis.md`.
3. **"Mira" vs. "Nina."** In Chapter IX the apparition underlines Luke 12 in a book at Vance Hall and writes "Mira." In Chapter XX the same apparition (Dianthe says "I have seen her thus once before") underlines the same verse in the old family Bible at Livingston Hall and writes "Nina." The 1x20 script keeps both names.
4. **When Hallow-eve falls.** Chapter I opens "the first week in November" and the concert follows that evening; Chapter III's Hallow-eve follows the concert, saying Reuel's restlessness began "three weeks before," and in Chapter V Reuel says he first saw Dianthe seven weeks before 24 December. Hallow-eve (31 October) cannot come after the first week of November.
5. **How old Aubrey was.** In Chapter VI he remembers, "very small" but able to recall it, a dinner two years before the war at which Mira, his mother's waiting-maid, prophesied. In Chapter XXI Hannah says he was born a week before Mira was to be sold, and Chapter VI says she was sold "a few months before the secession."
6. **Half or full siblings.** Hannah calls Aubrey Dianthe's "own blood brother" and the three children are all Mira's; Jim Titus, dying, calls Dianthe "the half-sister of Aubrey Livingston, who is your half-brother."
7. **Small physical details.** Aubrey's hair is "fair" (III) and "bright" (V), then "auburn" (XXIV); Dianthe's hair is chestnut (II), "golden" (I, XX), and her eyes brown (I, II) and then "deep grey" (XX).
8. **Calendar strain in Africa.** The expedition sails on 1 July and Charlie says in Chapter XVIII that they have been "romping around for almost six months" while Jim dies two months after Reuel's disappearance; Chapter XX says that same night Aubrey and Dianthe had "just returned" to Maryland, before the early-November scene there. The novel's own arithmetic doesn't close.
