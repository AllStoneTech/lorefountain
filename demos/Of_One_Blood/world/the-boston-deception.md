Aubrey Livingston's scheme against Reuel and Dianthe, from the concert where the three first come together through the canoe accident, and on to Jim Titus's confession, the coerced marriage, and Aubrey's reckoning. Deliberately non-contiguous with [[The Search for Telassar]] — it goes quiet for episodes `1x10`–`1x18` while Reuel is in Africa, then resumes and resolves alongside it in `1x19`, the same way a returning subplot works in any serialized drama.
