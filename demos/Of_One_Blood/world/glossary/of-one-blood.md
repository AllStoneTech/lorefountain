---
term: Of one blood
gloss: "From Acts 17:26 (\"[God] hath made of one blood all nations of men\") — the novel's title phrase and its explicit answer to the era's racist pseudoscience."
aliases: []
tags: [theme, scripture]
---

Used earnestly, not ironically: the novel's central claim is that Reuel, Dianthe, and Aubrey being siblings — and Boston and [[Telassar]] being connected by blood at all — is the rule, not the exception, of how humanity actually works.
