---
term: Jubilee Singers
gloss: Touring Black choral ensembles (after the real Fisk Jubilee Singers) who performed spirituals to raise money for Black colleges — Dianthe's troupe before the train wreck.
aliases: []
tags: [period-term, music]
---

[[Dianthe Lusk]] is introduced as a soloist with a Fisk-affiliated jubilee troupe performing "Go Down, Moses" at [[Tremont Temple]] — the concert where [[Reuel Briggs]] first hears her. The same song recurs twice more across the story, each time at a moment when her buried memory or identity is breaking through.
