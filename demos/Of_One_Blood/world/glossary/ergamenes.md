---
term: Ergamenes
gloss: The ancient king, in the reign of the second Ptolemy, whose returning heir the camel-driver told Professor Stone would restore the hidden city — the name Reuel is crowned and hailed under.
aliases: [King Ergamenes]
tags: [telassar, prophecy]
---

The prophecy [[Professor Stone]] relates names "an offspring of that Ergamenes who lived in the reign of the second Ptolemy"; "Ergamenes" becomes [[Reuel Briggs]]'s throne name when Telassar's people crown him and hail him in the great temple — "HAIL! ERGAMENES!" — and Ai later names the lotus lily on his breast as God's mark of his race and descent.
