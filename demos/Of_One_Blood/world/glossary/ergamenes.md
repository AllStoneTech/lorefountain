Named for a real ancient Meroitic king in the prophecy carried by [[Professor Stone's Parchment]], "Ergamenes" becomes [[Reuel Briggs]]'s throne name the moment Telassar's people recognize his lotus-lily birthmark and hail him in the great temple: "HAIL! ERGAMENES!"
