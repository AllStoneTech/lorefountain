The elder Dr. Livingston, for whom his own son is named — a slaveholding physician-scientist who studied mesmeric phenomena using [[Mira]] as an unwilling subject, fathered her children, and, once his white wife learned of them, sold Mira away rather than acknowledge them. He never learns that the "legitimate" son he raised in her place is actually Mira's own child, swapped at birth by [[Hannah]].
