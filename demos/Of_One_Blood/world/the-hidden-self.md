The novel's subtitle and its organizing idea, spoken aloud by Reuel himself in the opening chapter: "the wonders of a material world cannot approach those of the undiscovered country within ourselves — the hidden self lying quiescent in every human soul." [[Reuel Briggs]] hides both his ancestry and his mesmeric gift; [[Dianthe Lusk]] literally loses access to her own identity after the train wreck; [[Aubrey Livingston]] hides his crimes behind a friend's face and never learns that his own hidden self — Mira's blood, swapped into the Livingston line at birth — is the deepest concealment in the whole book. The concept ties Hopkins's own psychical-research interests to her argument about racial identity: the self hidden by passing and the self mesmerism claims to uncover are treated as the same kind of hidden thing.
