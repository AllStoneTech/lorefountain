A jubilee soloist whose train is wrecked on tour; taken for dead, she's revived by [[Reuel Briggs]]'s mesmeric gift but left with no memory of who she was. She and Reuel marry the morning he leaves for [[Meroe]]; in his absence a canoe accident on the [[Charles River]] drowns [[Molly Vance]] and leaves Dianthe presumed dead too, though [[Aubrey Livingston]] has actually pulled her from the water and hidden her away. Told Reuel is dead, she's coerced into marrying Aubrey — until intercepted mail proves he orchestrated Reuel's ruin, and her own grandmother, [[Hannah]], reveals that she, Reuel, and Aubrey share one mother. She tries to poison Aubrey herself; he forces her to drink it instead, and she dies the night Reuel finally makes it home.
