The ancient ruined city on the Nile in Nubia (present-day Sudan) that an English-led archaeological expedition — [[Reuel Briggs]] signed on as physician through a connection [[Aubrey Livingston]] arranged, accompanied by [[Charlie Vance]] and [[Jim Titus]], scholarly direction from [[Professor Stone]] — travels to excavate after outfitting at [[Tripoli]]. Its ruins are the surface; [[Telassar]] is what's actually buried beneath them.
