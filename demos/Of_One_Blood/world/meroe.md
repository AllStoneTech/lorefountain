The ancient ruined city on the Nile — "the island of Meroe," ringed by tributaries of the river, with some eighty pyramids "in a state of partial preservation" — that an English-led expedition — [[Reuel Briggs]] signed on as physician through a connection [[Aubrey Livingston]] arranged, accompanied by [[Charlie Vance]] and [[Jim Titus]], scholarly direction from [[Professor Stone]] — travels to excavate after outfitting at [[Tripoli]]. Its ruins are the surface; the passage to [[Telassar]] lies beneath the third great pyramid.
