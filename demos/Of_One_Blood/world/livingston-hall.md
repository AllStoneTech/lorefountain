Where [[Aubrey Livingston]] brings [[Dianthe Lusk]] after their wedding, explicitly called his "ancestral home" and placed in Maryland — which sits uneasily against [[Livingston Plantation]]/Laurel Hill being just as explicitly placed in Virginia elsewhere in the story. Marked `canon_status: contradicted` rather than silently resolved in one direction; both locations are modeled as the novel actually presents them. This is where Dianthe is coerced into marriage, discovers Jim Titus's letter, meets Aunt Hannah, and dies.
