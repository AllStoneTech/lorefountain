A ring of dull gold set with a single gem cut in the shape of a lotus lily — the same mark every one of Mira's children carries as a birthmark. [[Queen Candace]] places it on [[Reuel Briggs]]'s hand herself at their betrothal ceremony, sealing their union: "Thus do I claim thee for all eternity."
