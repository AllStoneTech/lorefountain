Reuel's closest friend at Harvard and, on the surface, his benefactor — it's Aubrey's money that funds the [[Meroe]] expedition, and Aubrey who has quietly known Reuel's ancestry, without ever saying so, since the day they met. What no one including Aubrey knows is that he is himself [[Mira]]'s son, swapped at birth for the Livingstons' own dying infant by [[Hannah]] to save him from being sold away. Engaged to [[Molly Vance]] while obsessed with [[Dianthe Lusk]], he lets Molly drown in a canoe accident he could have prevented, secretly saves and hides Dianthe, and — once Reuel is safely in Africa — pays [[Jim Titus]] to sabotage and, if he can manage it, kill him. He tells Dianthe Reuel is dead and marries her by fraud. When the truth of their shared blood finally reaches him, ancient Telassar law does what no American court will: compelled by [[Ai]]'s hypnotic sentence, he drowns himself in the same stretch of the [[Charles River]] that took Molly.
