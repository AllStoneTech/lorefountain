The hidden city of refuge four days' journey from [[Meroe]] "toward Arabia," on a forked tributary among high mountains and swamps (an outer city and an inner city within two walls), reached by a passage beneath the third great pyramid. Its people — Ai calls them "the direct descendants of the inhabitants of Meroe" — kept their ancient records and their learning intact, "behind the protection of our mountains and swamps," while certain Council members visit the outside world. Reuel is brought here after his disappearance in the pyramid, is crowned its king, and later returns to rule it. The novel ends with the uneasy sense that the wider world is closing in on it.
