A six-thousand-year-old city hidden beneath the ruins of [[Meroe]], populated by the descendants of ancient Meroë/Kush who kept both their mysticism and an advanced civilization intact, unknown to the outside world. Reuel is brought here after his disappearance at the dig site, discovers it's the seat of his own bloodline, and is crowned its king. The novel ends with the uneasy sense that the wider world is getting closer to finding it.
