The camel-driver hired to guide the expedition across the desert toward Meroe — who turns out to be a member of Telassar's Council, sent to watch the party from the moment they landed and to report Reuel's arrival as the prophesied king. Marked `canon_status: contradicted`: later chapters twice refer to this same character as "Abdallah," which is also the name explicitly given to an unrelated Tripoli sheikh earlier in the story — an inconsistency in the source text itself, not resolved here rather than silently smoothed over.
