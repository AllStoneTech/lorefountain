The camel-driver introduced to the expedition by Sheik Mohammed Abdallah in Tripoli to lead the party across the desert toward Meroe — who turns out to be a member of Telassar's Council, the one who "brought us news of your arrival, and the intention of the expedition." Marked `canon_status: contradicted`: in Chapters XVIII and XIX this same character is called "Abdallah" five times (Chapter XVIII "Ai and Abdallah and Jim"; Chapter XIX "Ai and Abdallah in the guise of servants"), while Chapters X, XIII, XV and XXIV call him Ababdis; "Abdallah" is also the name explicitly given to an unrelated Tripoli sheikh earlier in the story. A transcriber's note in the Gutenberg text records correcting one misprint, "Abdadis," to "Ababdis" in Chapter XXIV. The scripts use "Ababdis" throughout; the inconsistency is in the source text itself, noted here rather than silently smoothed over.
