Two linked instruments in Ai's private chamber: a polished disk that reflects the past, and a stone font of water that reflects the present (and, specially prepared, a single vision of it). Ai uses them to show [[Reuel Briggs]] the truth of the canoe accident, that [[Dianthe Lusk]] is alive and in [[Aubrey Livingston]]'s power, and that [[Molly Vance]] is dead — the moment Reuel stops believing the false news from home.
