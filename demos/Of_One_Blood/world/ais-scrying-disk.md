Two linked instruments in Ai's plain underground chamber: a polished disk that reflects the past (and, if washed with a special preparation and left to cake and be scraped and polished, a single picture of the present that stays for a number of days), and a stone font of water that reflects the living and the future. Ai uses them to show [[Reuel Briggs]] the canoe with Aubrey, Molly and Dianthe, [[Molly Vance]] dead in the river, [[Dianthe Lusk]] alive in the font, and finally — on the prepared disk — Aubrey and Dianthe together at a window in the Livingstons' ancestral home, the moment Reuel stops believing the news from home.
