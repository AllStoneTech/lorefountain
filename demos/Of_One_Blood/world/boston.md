The novel's home base: Reuel's hospital and lecture halls, the [[Tremont Temple]] concert, and the social world Aubrey and the Vances move through. Neighboring Mount Auburn — home to the [[Vance Estate]] and [[Hyde House]] — sits just outside the city proper, but the story treats "Boston and vicinity" as one social world throughout. Everything here reads as ordinary, respectable Gilded Age propriety right up until the story starts pulling at what it's built on.
