James Vance's daughter and Charlie's sister, hostess of the [[Vance Estate]] where [[Dianthe Lusk]] recovers and, unknowingly, engaged to a man who will let her die rather than lose the woman he actually wants. When their canoe capsizes on the [[Charles River]], Aubrey frees himself from her grasp to save Dianthe instead; only Molly's body is ever recovered, and her death is allowed to pass as a tragic accident.
