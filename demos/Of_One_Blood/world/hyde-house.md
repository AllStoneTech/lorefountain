A reputedly haunted house in Mount Auburn, its grounds adjoining the [[Vance Estate]] and standing nearly opposite Mount Auburn cemetery. Local legend holds that a lady in white haunts its avenue of hemlocks on Hallow-eve when the moon is new; daring each other to test it is what puts [[Reuel Briggs]] alone in that avenue face to face with [[Dianthe Lusk]] for the first time.
