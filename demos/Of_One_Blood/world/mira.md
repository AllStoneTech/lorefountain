[[Hannah]]'s daughter — the only one of her ten children "massa" left her — enslaved on the [[Livingston Plantation]], where she served the doctor's wife as waiting-maid and the doctor threw her into a mesmeric trance to read minds and prophesy for his guests. Her three children, [[Reuel Briggs]], [[Dianthe Lusk]] (the first two named by Hannah) and a third son, raised as [[Aubrey Livingston]], share one mother and, by Hannah's account, the doctor as father; Hannah says each bears the lotus-lily mark. Hannah calls Aubrey Dianthe's "own blood brother"; Jim Titus, dying, says "half." Sold away once the doctor's white wife learned of the children, Mira disappears from the record entirely — but not from Reuel's or Dianthe's visions, where her ghost keeps trying to make the truth known long before any living person will say it aloud. Her descent from the [[House of Telassar]]'s royal line is implied rather than stated: the novel says only that the lotus-lily mark proves royal blood and that Aubrey, as Mira's son, falls under the law for the royal line.
