The last unknowing daughter of the [[House of Telassar]]'s royal line, enslaved at [[Livingston Plantation]] and — like her own mother, [[Hannah]] — made to perform mesmeric "parlor tricks and prophecies" for her enslaver's guests. All three of her children, [[Reuel Briggs]], [[Dianthe Lusk]], and [[Aubrey Livingston]], carry both the same father and the same lotus-lily birthmark; none of them know it until decades later. Sold away once her enslaver's wife learned of the children, Mira disappears from the historical record entirely — but not, it turns out, from either Reuel's or Dianthe's dreams, where her ghost keeps trying to make the truth known long before any living person will say it aloud.
