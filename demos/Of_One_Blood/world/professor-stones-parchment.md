A faded Arabic parchment and chart given to [[Professor Stone]] twenty-five years earlier by a dying camel-driver, describing Meroe's hidden mines and the swamp-hidden city beyond them, guarded by a sacred crocodile and jewel-crowned serpents. It also carries the prophecy that drives the novel's second half: that an heir of the ancient king Ergamenes, descended from Ethiopians carried off as slaves to lands beyond the sea, will one day return and restore the hidden city's glory.
