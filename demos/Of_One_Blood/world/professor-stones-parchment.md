A faded Arabic parchment and chart, sealed in an envelope with a lotus-shaped black seal, given to [[Professor Stone]] twenty-five years earlier by a camel-driver who sustained a fatal accident. It describes the mines four days from Meroe toward Arabia and the swamp-hidden city beyond them, guarded by a sacred crocodile and jewel-crowned serpents, and a secret passage under the third great pyramid. The prophecy that drives the novel's second half is not on the parchment: Stone says the camel-driver "added a fact which the manuscript withholds" — that an heir of the ancient king Ergamenes, descended from Ethiopians carried off as slaves to lands beyond unknown seas, would one day return and restore the hidden city's glory.
