Part of the Boston social circle around the Vances, Charlie Vance's partner in mischief and the girl he shared "the jollities of the past few months" with before sailing. Charlie, who had sworn he'd be "drawn and quartered before he'd tie up to a girl of the period," resolves in the Telassar cell to go home and marry her.
