The elder Dr. Livingston's Virginia plantation, called Laurel Hill, where [[Mira]] and her mother [[Hannah]] were enslaved, and where all three of Mira's children were born. Confirmed as Virginia by two details: Aubrey's own statement that he'll "renew the ancient splendor of his ancestral home" there after marrying, and the Laurel Hill, Virginia address he gives when he secretly recruits [[Jim Titus]]. Compare [[Livingston Hall]], the "ancestral home" the story later places in Maryland — likely the same family's other property, though the text never reconciles the two directly.
