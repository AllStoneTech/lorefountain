Aubrey's wealth buys lawyers against the Vances' and Reuel's charges, and "the dead tell no tales" without lawful testimony. Instead, Ai places him in a hypnotic trance and whispers a command — "death by thine own hand" — the sentence Telassar's ancient laws impose on a member of the royal family in direct line guilty of murder: he becomes his own executioner. Aubrey is later found drowned in the same stretch of the Charles River that took Molly. Reuel returns to Telassar with Hannah to rule beside Candace.
