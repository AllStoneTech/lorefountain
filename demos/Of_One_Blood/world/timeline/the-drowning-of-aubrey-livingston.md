Legal charges against Aubrey collapse under his own lawyers. Instead, Ai places him in a hypnotic trance and pronounces Telassar's ancient sentence on a member of the royal line guilty of murder: he must be his own executioner. Aubrey is later found drowned in the same stretch of the Charles River that took Molly. Reuel returns to Telassar with Hannah to rule beside Candace.
