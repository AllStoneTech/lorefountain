Mira's third child, a son, is born the same night the Livingstons' own infant by the elder Dr. Livingston's white wife dies within the hour. In grief and desperation, Hannah swaps the two — Mira's son is raised as the Livingstons' legitimate heir, [[Aubrey Livingston]], while Mira's actual dead child is passed off as hers. Not revealed to any of the three siblings until `1x21`.
