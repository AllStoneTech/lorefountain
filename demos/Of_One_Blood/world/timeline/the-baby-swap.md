Mira's third child, a son, is born at about the same time as the child of the elder Dr. Livingston's white wife, who dies within the hour. In the excitement, with Hannah alone when the child dies and the house wild for fear the wife would die, Hannah swaps the two and tells Marse Livingston that Mira's boy was the dead one — Mira's son is raised as the Livingstons' legitimate heir, [[Aubrey Livingston]]. Not revealed to any of the three siblings until `1x21`. (Chapter VI has Aubrey remembering Mira as his mother's waiting-maid at a dinner two years before the war; the novel never reconciles that with a birth a week before her sale.)
