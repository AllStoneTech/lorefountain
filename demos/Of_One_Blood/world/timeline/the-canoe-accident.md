A canoe carrying Aubrey, Molly, and Dianthe capsizes on the Charles River when Aubrey's paddle catches in the lily stems; a call for help is heard, then silence (Chapter IX). Not until Chapter XX (`1x20`) does the novel show that Aubrey kicked free of Molly's clinging arms to save Dianthe, left Molly to sink — her body later surfacing "as did poor Elaine" — and hid Dianthe in a cottage, while the world believed both girls drowned.
