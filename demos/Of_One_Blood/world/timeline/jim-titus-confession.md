Crushed by the serpents, Jim Titus confesses to Reuel that Aubrey paid him to put him "out of the way by accident" — and that Dianthe is Reuel's own sister, the half-sister of Aubrey, who is Reuel's half-brother; Reuel answers "I am Mira's son." Reuel sails for America within the month.
