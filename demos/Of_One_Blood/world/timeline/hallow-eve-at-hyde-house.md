Daring each other to test Hyde House's ghost legend, Reuel walks alone in its avenue and meets a figure with Dianthe's face — sorrowful, gazing as if she sees nothing — who tells him only "You can help me, but not now; tomorrow" before disappearing among the hemlocks. He tells Aubrey later he spoke to her "Hallow-eve ... in the garden of the haunted house, but not in the flesh." (Chapter III places Hallow-eve after a concert dated the first week of November.)
