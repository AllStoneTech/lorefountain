Exploring a ruined desert castle with only Jim Titus (waiting below with the guns) for company, Reuel is stalked by a leopard on a ledge seven hundred feet above the plain; Jim doesn't answer his cries — Charlie finds him "apparently dead with sleep" — and Charlie Vance arrives just in time with Reuel's own revolver to save him.
