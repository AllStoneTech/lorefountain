At a plantation dinner party, the elder Dr. Livingston has Mira, entranced, tell the company what she sees; she foretells widows, early graves for the men, and "for you, captain, a prison cell and a pauper's grave." It comes true — the doctor dies a prisoner of war in Boston Harbor and every woman at the table is widowed; he can no longer abide her and sells her "just a few months before the secession." Aubrey tells this story himself at Christmas in `1x06`, with nothing in the text to suggest he knows his own part in it.
