At a plantation dinner party, the elder Dr. Livingston has Mira perform one of her mesmeric "tricks" for his guests; instead she delivers an unscripted doom prophecy — every man dead or imprisoned, every woman a widow. It comes true down to the letter; her enslaver can no longer abide her and sells her away not long after. Aubrey tells this story himself at Christmas in `1x06`, with no idea he is the very child born into it.
