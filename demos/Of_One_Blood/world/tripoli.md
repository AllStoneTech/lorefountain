The Mediterranean port city where the expedition lands, hires camels and drivers (including [[Ababdis]]) from Sheik Mohammed Abdallah, and outfits itself for the desert crossing toward [[Meroe]]. The month-long wait here for the Sheik's audience is the last stretch of the journey with reliable mail from home.
