The Mediterranean port city where the expedition lands and waits "weeks, and a month finally rolled into oblivion" for an audience with Sheik Mohammed Abdallah. He introduces [[Ababdis]], an owner of camels, and the party outfits itself with fifteen camels and their drivers for the desert crossing toward [[Meroe]]. No letters from America come for Reuel or Charlie during the wait, though Jim and everyone else receive mail.
