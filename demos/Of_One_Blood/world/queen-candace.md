Telassar's reigning queen, one in an unbroken line of women all named Candace, each chosen from the city's virgins to govern in the royal bloodline's long absence. Strikingly similar in appearance to [[Dianthe Lusk]], she and [[Reuel Briggs]] are formally betrothed within days of his arrival, sealed with a lotus-lily ring she places on his hand herself; the marriage follows once he returns from America for good.
