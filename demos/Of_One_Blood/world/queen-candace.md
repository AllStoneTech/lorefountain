Telassar's reigning virgin queen, one in a line of women all named Candace, each chosen from the city's virgins to reign until the prophesied Ergamenes comes to inaugurate a dynasty of kings. Strikingly similar in appearance to [[Dianthe Lusk]], she and [[Reuel Briggs]] are formally betrothed soon after his coronation, sealed with a lotus-lily ring she places on his hand herself; the wedding is set for a month later, and the last chapter finds Reuel "united to Candace" after his return from America.
