The English scholar who leads the Meroe expedition, carrying a chart and parchment given to him twenty-five years earlier by a camel-driver who had a fatal accident. His life's theory — that Ethiopia, not Egypt, is civilization's true origin point — drives the whole journey; he gets his excavation and his treasure (shipped to England's Society of Geographical Research), but cannot move the Sphinx's head, and the novel never shows him learning that the prophecy the camel-driver told him comes true in the person of his own expedition's doctor.
