The English scholar who leads the Meroe expedition, carrying a chart and prophecy-parchment given to him twenty-five years earlier by a dying camel-driver. His life's theory — that Ethiopia, not Egypt, is civilization's true origin point — drives the whole journey; he gets his excavation, his treasure, and his vindication, but never learns that the prophecy his own parchment describes comes true in the person of his own expedition's doctor.
