A narrator invented for the audio adaptation. Du Bois's prose is third person and heavily interior; much of it is given to this voice, almost entirely in Du Bois's own sentences, and the rest to action, sound and the leads' own speech. This entity is `tentative`: it exists only in the audio drama.
