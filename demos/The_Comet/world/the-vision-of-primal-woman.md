Julia's moment on the roof: "She was no mere woman. She was neither high nor low, white nor black, rich nor poor. She was primal woman; mighty mother of all men to come and Bride of Life." She sees Jim as "her Brother Humanity incarnate, Son of God and great All-Father of the race to be." The text states it from inside her: Jim does not glimpse it, and she never speaks it aloud.
