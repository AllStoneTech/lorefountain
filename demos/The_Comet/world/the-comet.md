A new comet, "quite a stranger," not Halley's. The city is talking of it all morning: the president saw it the night before and is delighted; Julia photographed it the night before and was developing the pictures in her dark room when it passed; the vault clerk says its tail "hits us at noon." The "last edition" warns of "deadly gases." It passes (Jim says "this morning"; the street is silent at "high-noon") and the city dies; the survivors we meet were underground or shut in a dark room, which is the story's implication, not a stated cause. Jim calls it "comet or devil." At twilight it returns to the sky as "a long, white star--mystic, wonderful!" with "a pale, wide sheet of flame" streaming up like a bridal veil.

The text never says what the poison is, and never says that anything outside New York has been affected; Fred's "Only New York" is the only line about its extent.
