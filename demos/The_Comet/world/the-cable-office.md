Reached by flying "to the cable office on the east side, leaving the world of wealth and prosperity for the world of poverty and work." "Great, dark coils of wire came up from the earth and down from the sun and entered this low lair of witchery." Black water laps far below. Jim knows "the call for help--we used it formerly at the bank" and steps within; the text does not say whether he sends it. Julia waits, then finds him beside the water with his coat off. "The world lies beneath the waters now--may I go?" "No."
