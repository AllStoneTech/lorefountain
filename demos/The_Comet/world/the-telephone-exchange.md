The "central telephone exchange," reached "in fifteen minutes." Jim steps before Julia at the door, "pressed her gently back as he closed it," and she hears him "moving to and fro" with "the poor, little burdens he bore" (the text implies he clears away the dead; it does not say so). A hall beyond is "filled with dead women." An alley at the side lets out onto the street.
