The wall between rich and poor, white and Black, that stood the day before: the restaurant that "would not have served me"; the woman who "would scarcely have looked at him twice"; Jim as "dirt beneath her silken feet." Julia says on the roof: "how foolish our human distinctions seem--now." Jim says: "I was not--human, yesterday." The story is the brief eclipse of this wall and its return.
