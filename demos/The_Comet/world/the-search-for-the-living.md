The sweep through the dead city: Jim alone from the bank to Fifth Avenue, then the two of them "from Madison Square to Spuyten Duyvel," over the Williamsburg Bridge and Brooklyn, to Battery and Morningside Heights, then the telephone, the cable, and the rockets. Each step fails, until the answer comes from below.
