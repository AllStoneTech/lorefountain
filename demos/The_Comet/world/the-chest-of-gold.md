An old, strong, rusty iron chest at the far end of the hidden vault, its hinges "deeply incrusted with rust. The rust had eaten a hundred years." Jim pries the lid up and sees "the dull sheen of gold!" Then the door closes and he forgets it. The story never mentions it again, and never says what becomes of it. (The adaptation gives Jim one invented voice-over thought of it in 1x02; see `world/notes/who-is-the-chest-for.md`.)
