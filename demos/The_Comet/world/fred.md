A younger man in motor costume who arrives with Julia's father and bends over her "with passionate solicitude." He is the owner of the new Mercedes in which her father drove off that morning. On finding Jim beside her, his hand flies to his hip and he uses the slur. Julia answers: "He has dared--all, to rescue me, and I--thank him--much."
