term: Negro
gloss: The period term the source uses, in the narrator's voice, for a Black person; alongside "colored man" and, in the mouths of Fred and the crowd, a racial slur. The adaptation keeps all of them as written.
aliases: [colored, the colored man]

---

Du Bois's narrator writes "Negro" and "the colored man" (the standard terms of respect in 1920) and hands the slur to Fred and to the crowd on the roof, where it marks them. The scripts keep every use in place and give the slur only to those characters. See the content note in the README and `imports/source-material.md`; `world/notes/` has the production question about delivery.
