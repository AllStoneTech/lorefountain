---
term: Friedhof
gloss: German for a churchyard or cemetery; Du Bois's word for the "solemn, world-wide" graveyard the two survivors seem to be moving through.
aliases: []
tags: [language, german]

Italicized in the source ("_Friedhof_"). Pronunciation for performers: FREED-hohf. In the audio drama it is spoken once, by the Narrator, in 1x04, in the passage where the dead become sleepers.
