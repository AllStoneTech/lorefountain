---
term: Spuyten Duyvel
gloss: The creek and neighborhood at the northern tip of Manhattan; the far end of the survivors' search ("from Madison Square to Spuyten Duyvel").
aliases: []
tags: [geography]
---

Pronounced SPY-ten DY-vul. Named in the source only as the northern limit of the search. The audio drama gives it a place-name read by the Narrator and nothing else.
