---
term: deadly gases
gloss: The cause of death the story's newspaper expects from the comet's tail ("Deadly gases expected. Close doors and windows. Seek the cellar."); the text never confirms it.
aliases: [the gas, the odor]
tags: [catastrophe, ambiguity]
---

Jim's own words are "Something--comet or devil." The text offers only a "strong, peculiar odor" in the vault and the "sickening stench" later, and a newspaper headline. For production: never name the gas aloud outside the headline. The 1910 scare was about cyanogen, a real gas found in comet spectra; that detail is outside the story and is not used in the scripts.
