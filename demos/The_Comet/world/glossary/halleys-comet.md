---
term: Halley's Comet
gloss: The periodic comet whose tail the Earth passed through in May 1910; the story's junior clerk recalls "journeying through the comet's tail once," and the president corrects him that this new comet is a stranger.
aliases: [Halley's]
tags: [astronomy, historical-context]
---

The source text names only "Halley's." The 1910 passage and the public alarm about poisonous gas in the comet's tail is real history outside the text; it explains why the clerk's remark is made lightly, and why the president can say "Are you scared?" with a smile. The story's new comet is fictional.
