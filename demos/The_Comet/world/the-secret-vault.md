A long, narrow room with shelves behind a wall that swings "as on mighty hinges" at the far end of the old vault: "some hiding place of the old bank unknown in newer times." At its far end sits an old iron chest. On a high shelf lie the two missing record volumes and others. Its stone door swings shut behind Jim ("Boom!") and he must find the metal catch by touch to get out, which is how he survives the comet's passage.
