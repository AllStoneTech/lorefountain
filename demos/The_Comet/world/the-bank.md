The bank where Jim works as messenger, entered from the steps on Broadway and, by a side door, from Wall Street. The text never names it; it is "the bank," with a president, a junior clerk, a vault clerk, a watchman and guards, and vaults reaching several levels below the street (a basement, a sub-vault, a "lowest cavern"); the text gives no count. Its old vaults are seeping water. Everything of value has been moved out of the lower vaults, except one thing no one remembers.
