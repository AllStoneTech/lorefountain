The bank's president, who asks Jim "Well, Jim, are you scared?" and then tells him to go down to the lower vaults, since "we miss two volumes of old records." He saw the comet the night before and calls it "wonderful, wonderful." Jim finds him dead on his desk, "the twisted, oozing mouth of the president where he lay half-buried."
