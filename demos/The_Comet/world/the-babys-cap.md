"Something film-like" in Jim's hand when he returns from Harlem, which he stuffs into his pocket. Only on the tower platform, in the glare of the lights, does he bring it out: "a baby's filmy cap, and gazed again." The text never explains it; the audience puts it together a beat before the woman with the dead baby arrives.
