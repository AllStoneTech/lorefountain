The small, brown, toil-worn woman who climbs onto the tower platform with a dead baby in her arm, sees Jim, and cries "Jim!" The source never names her or calls her his wife; that she is his wife (and the baby their child) is the clear implication of "Everybody," of the baby's cap in his pocket, and of her cry, so the entity is `tentative`.
