Jim walks up Fifth Avenue to the "famous hostelry"; later, driving the Ford up the street, he detours to Park Avenue at 42nd Street to avoid "the dead congestion," back onto Fifth at 57th, past the Plaza and the park "with its hushed babies and silent throng," to 72nd Street, where a living voice calls from a window. It is also the road down which he and Julia whirl from Harlem to the tower.
