What Jim carries from Harlem: "I have lost--everybody." Never spoken of again, but in his pocket through the car ride, the cable office and the roof, until the lights come on and he takes it out in front of strangers. The arc is the one the adaptation keeps off the microphone: his grief is heard as breathing and silence, not as speech.
