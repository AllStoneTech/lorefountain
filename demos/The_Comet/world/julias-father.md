Julia's father, known only by the initials J.B.H. at the foot of the note he left on his desk. He drove a hundred miles out in Fred's new Mercedes that morning, "shall not be back before dinner," and so was beyond the comet's reach. He returns with Fred and a crowd to the tower, takes his daughter in his arms, and presses a roll of bills into Jim's hands: "Well, Jim, I thank you. I've always liked your people. If you ever want a job, call on me."
