The moment the two survivors stop seeing death and start seeing sleep: "They seemed to move in a world silent and asleep,--not dead. They moved in quiet reverence, lest somehow they wake these sleeping forms who had, at last, found peace. They moved in some solemn, world-wide _Friedhof_" (German for churchyard or cemetery). The audio adaptation reflects the shift in how silence itself is mixed.
