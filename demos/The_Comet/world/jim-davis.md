Bank messenger on Broadway, a tall, dark workingman who knows exactly where the world puts him: "outside the world... 'nothing!'" He is the one sent down into the flooded lower vaults because it is "too dangerous for more valuable men," and that errand is what saves his life. He wakes to a silent New York, searches it for the living, finds [[Julia]], takes his turn at the wheel, and goes to Harlem to learn what he has lost. At the water's edge he asks whether he may go; she says no. He is thanked and tipped by a rescuer who "always liked your people," and is restored to the one person left who is his own.

His name is given only at the very end, when the white-haired man asks: "Jim Davis."
