The newsboy's last paper: "'Danger!' screamed its black headlines. 'Warnings wired around the world. The Comet's tail sweeps past us at noon. Deadly gases expected. Close doors and windows. Seek the cellar.'" Jim reads it and staggers on: the warning that had been out all morning.
