A note "folded and addressed but unsent" on the desk in the Metropolitan Tower office: "Dear Daughter: I've gone for a hundred mile spin in Fred's new Mercedes. Shall not be back before dinner. I'll bring Fred with me. J.B.H." It suggests to them both that he may be alive somewhere, away from the office (an inference; the note says only that he has gone for a drive).
