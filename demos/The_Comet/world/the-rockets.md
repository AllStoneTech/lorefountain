Jim's last means of calling the world, after the telephone and cable fail: "night rockets and then--flight!" On the tower roof he sends one up that "arose, shrieked, and flew up, a slim path of light," and then rocket after rocket "into the unanswering darkness." Then a rocket "blazed into the air" from almost at their feet, and Jim's next rocket is the "answering" one.
