# What the comet did

Open question the text refuses to close. The headline says "deadly gases expected"; Jim says "comet or devil"; the vault has a "strong, peculiar odor." Fred says "Only New York," but we don't know how he knows, or that this is true. Julia asks "is the world gone?" and the story never answers for the rest of the world.

For production: keep the cause unexplained in the sound design. The aftermath (silence, the crush in the doorway, the bodies in their last motions) carries the story. Never add a narrator line that names the poison.
