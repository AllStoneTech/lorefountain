# Running time

The story is roughly 6,300 words, about 45 minutes read aloud at an audiobook pace, much of it interior description. Six episodes of 10-15 minutes each would run an hour or more because of silence and sound beats, so the drama should be paced by sound: a long vault descent, the 1x02 walk, a full minute of dead-line switchboard in 1x04, and the Tower elevators at the end of 1x06. The scripts here are drafts of the scenes and cues, not a stopwatch; first table-read will tell which episodes (probably 1x05 and 1x06) are short.

Episodes 1x05 and 1x06 are the shortest in source text and may be merged into one if the table read is brisk.
