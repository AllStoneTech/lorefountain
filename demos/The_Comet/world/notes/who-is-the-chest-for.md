# What was the chest for?

The hidden vault, the old chest, and the gold ("the rust had eaten a hundred years") are never mentioned again after the door slams. Du Bois uses them to explain why Jim is below ground, not to set up a payoff. The audio drama should not pay them off either. Question for the writers' room: do we let Jim think of it once, in 1x02, after the silence (a flicker of the gold, at his wits' end) so the thread closes honestly without inventing a use? The draft does so with one invented line of voice-over for Jim (1x02), flagged in `imports/source-material.md`.
