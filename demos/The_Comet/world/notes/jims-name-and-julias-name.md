# Names the text withholds

- Jim is "the messenger" until the white-haired man asks his name in the last pages.
- Julia's surname is never given; her father is "J.B.H."; Fred has no surname.
- The bank is never named; neither is the hostelry.
- Jim's wife and baby are never named.

The drama keeps every one of these as the story has them; introducing a name would break the effect of "Jim Davis" being asked for last.
