# How to deliver the slur

The source gives the slur to Fred (1x06) and, twice, to the crowd on the roof. The project rule is that it stays exactly as Du Bois wrote it: it is the point where the story's brief vision of equality is answered. Open production questions, not for the script:

- A spoken content advisory at the head of 1x06 (and in show notes), or at the head of the series?
- Who voices the crowd? The speeches are short and anonymous; casting them with the same actors who play Fred and the father makes the casting point that these are the same people who were thanking him a moment ago.
- Delivery: Fred's line is broken by his own breath ("It's--a--nigger--Julia!"). Direct it as a reflex, not a performance of hate.
- Do not bleep, and do not mask with a sound cue; that changes the source's emphasis.
