The whole stage of the story: from the bank on Broadway to the dead streets of Wall Street, Fifth Avenue and Harlem, from "Madison Square to Spuyten Duyvel," across the Williamsburg Bridge and Brooklyn, from the Battery to Morningside Heights. Fred's judgment of what the comet did: "Only New York."

The story places no year or date. Its details (a Ford, a Stutz, a Mercedes, an exchange of plug-in telephone cords, the Metropolitan Tower) point to the 1910s–1920s; the audio adaptation leaves the date unspoken.
