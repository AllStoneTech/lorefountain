The "human river that swirled down" outside the bank at the start, and the empty street the two survivors "puffed a third time slowly down... under the broiling sun" when the stench of the dead catches them. Jim's descent into the vaults is "down beneath Broadway, where the dim light filtered through the feet of hurrying men."
