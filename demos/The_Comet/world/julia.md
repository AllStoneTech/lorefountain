A rich young white woman, shut up in her dark room developing photographs of the comet while the world ended outside. She comes out of her dark room to the dead, sees them strewn before her window, and leans out of an upper window to cry for help into an empty street. A dead French maid and butler lie in her house. She can drive; he cannot. She will not let him die at the water's edge. On the roof she is overtaken by a vision of herself as "primal woman; mighty mother of all men to come and Bride of Life," and when the world returns she turns from him without looking back.

Her surname is never given; only her father's initials, J.B.H.
