A grimy newsboy found dead in the gutter, still holding up the "last edition" with its headlines: "Danger! Warnings wired around the world. The Comet's tail sweeps past us at noon. Deadly gases expected. Close doors and windows. Seek the cellar." Jim reads it and staggers on.
