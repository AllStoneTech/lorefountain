The "great, square" tower on Madison Square, where Julia's father keeps his office. An elevator boy lies dead; the office door stands open with a stenographer on the threshold and a clerk "staring at her"; the inner office holds the unsent note. At twilight Jim and Julia return, gather rugs and chairs, and climb to the roof, where they send up rockets and watch the comet and where, at the end, the elevators "shooting upward from below made the great tower tremble."
