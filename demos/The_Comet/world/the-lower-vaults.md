Down "beneath Broadway... to the dark basement beneath; down into the blackness and silence beneath that lowest cavern." Water has been seeping in; the floor is muddy, a rat leaps past, cobwebs cross the dark. Jim goes down with a dark lantern and a ring of keys, "in the bowels of the earth, under the world." Because he is down here, he lives.
