"The grim switchboard flashed its metallic face in cryptic, sphinx-like immobility." Julia sits on a stool, puts on "the bright earpiece," and studies the mouthpiece: "wide and black, pimpled with usage; inert; dead; almost sarcastic in its unfeeling curves." She calls to the world, and the world does not answer. At one point she thinks she hears a click ("Surely--no--was it the click of a receiver?"), and the text never confirms it.
