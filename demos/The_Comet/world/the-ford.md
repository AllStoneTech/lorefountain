The first car Jim takes: he "gently lifted off its burden," tested the throttle, found there was gas, and "glided off, shivering." It carries him up Fifth Avenue to 72nd Street. At the window he "wheeled the car in a sudden circle, running over the still body of a child and leaping on the curb."
