"A famous hostelry" on Fifth Avenue; its "gorgeous, ghost-haunted halls." Jim seizes a tray "from dead hands," takes it into the street and eats ravenously, hiding to keep out the sights: "Yesterday, they would not have served me." The text does not name the place; the audio drama does not either.
