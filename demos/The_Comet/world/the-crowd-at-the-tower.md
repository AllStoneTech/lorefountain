The men who rush up the elevators when the lights return. Their dialogue is a babel: "Who was it?" "Are they alive?" "How many?" "Two!" "Who was saved?" "A white girl and a nigger--there she goes." (The adaptation keeps the source's word; see the content note in the README.) Then one cries, "Let's lynch the damned----" and another answers, "Shut up--he's all right--he saved her"; a third: "Saved hell! He had no business----" Then "Here he comes," and Jim moves slowly into the glare of the lights. The story gives them no names.
