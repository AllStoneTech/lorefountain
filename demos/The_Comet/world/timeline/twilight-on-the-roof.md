Jim drives back at twilight; Julia half-wonders whether she has seen "the elf-queen" (she had been looking for her to "wave life into this dead world again"). In her father's office they gather rugs and chairs and Jim leaves a note on the desk. On the roof he makes her comfortable, feeds her, wraps her in a shawl. They speak: "Have you had to work hard?" "Always." "I was rich." "I was poor." "The rich and the poor are met together." "The Lord is the Maker of them all."
