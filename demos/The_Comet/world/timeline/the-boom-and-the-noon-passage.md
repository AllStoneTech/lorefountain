A "low, grinding, reverberating crash": the stone door swings shut. Jim searches for hours in the dark for a metal catch. While he is shut in, the comet's tail passes and the city dies; the story never describes this event, only its aftermath. (Jim later says the comet swept the earth "this morning" and finds the street silent at "high-noon," so the passage precedes noon in the story's own account; the vault clerk's "hits us at noon" was the forecast.)
