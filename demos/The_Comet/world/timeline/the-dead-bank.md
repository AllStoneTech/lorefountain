Jim wakes, finds a watchman "as if asleep" with the gate swinging free, a guard prostrate, and the whole bank silent with "the silent forms of men." He whispers "Robbery and murder," sees the president dead on his desk, and realizes that if he is found there alone with the money and the dead he will be blamed. He slips out into Wall Street.
