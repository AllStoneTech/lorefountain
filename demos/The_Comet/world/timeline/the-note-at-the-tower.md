At the Metropolitan Tower: a dead elevator boy, a stenographer on the threshold, a clerk staring at her, an empty inner office, and J.B.H.'s unsent note. Then the sweep from Madison Square to Spuyten Duyvel, over the Williamsburg Bridge and across Brooklyn, from the Battery and Morningside Heights, down Broadway a third time under the broiling sun.
