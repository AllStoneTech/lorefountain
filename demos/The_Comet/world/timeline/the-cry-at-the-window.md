Julia, leaning from an upper window, cries "help, in God's name!" Jim wheels the car and runs up the steps. Each is startled: she "had not noticed before that he was a Negro," and "he had not thought of her as white." She shows him the maid and the butler and tells him she was in her dark room developing photographs of the comet.
