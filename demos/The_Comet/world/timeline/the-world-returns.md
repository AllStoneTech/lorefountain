Men crowd the roof. Julia's father seizes his daughter; Fred reaches her, sees Jim, and reaches for his hip: "It's--a--nigger--Julia! Has he--has he dared----" Julia: "He has dared--all, to rescue me, and I--thank him--much." She does not look at Jim again. Her father presses money into his hands: "what's your name?" "Jim Davis." "I've always liked your people."
