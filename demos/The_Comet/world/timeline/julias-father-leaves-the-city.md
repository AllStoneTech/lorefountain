Before the comet's noon passage, Julia's father leaves a note on his desk in the Metropolitan Tower and goes off with Fred in Fred's new Mercedes for "a hundred mile spin," "Shall not be back before dinner." The text implies, but does not say outright, that being out of the city is why he and Fred are alive (Fred: "Only New York"). Learned by Jim and Julia in 1x03.
