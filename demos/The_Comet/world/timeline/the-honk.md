"Honk! Honk!" A motor sounds from the silence below, then a rocket blazes into the air almost from their feet. They start backward with eyes that "faltered and fell." Jim drops to his knees, gropes about the floor, and after an age a flame splutters and an answering rocket flies. They stand "looking to opposite ends of the earth." Then the elevators.
