Where Jim's nerve breaks: he runs, "shrieking and fighting the air," and sinks on the grass of Madison Square, then rises, goes to a fountain, bathes his face, and "thought the thing through." It is also where the two survivors return at twilight to the door of the Metropolitan Tower, and where their search begins ("They hunted from Madison Square to Spuyten Duyvel").
