The first place Jim insists on going: "No--first, we must go--to Harlem." Julia understands at once. They take the turn at 110th Street "on two wheels" and slip "with a shriek into 135th." He is gone a moment and returns gray-faced: "I have lost--everybody." He runs back, is gone "hours they seemed to her," and returns with "something film-like in his hand." Harlem then lies about them "among the dark and lined dead... the brown, still faces, the knotted hands, the homely garments, and the silence."
