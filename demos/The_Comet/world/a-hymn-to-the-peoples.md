The poem Du Bois sets after the story's last line. It is a prayer of all the peoples ("Pale and black, tawny, red and brown, / The mighty human rainbow of the world") to the "World-Spirit." The audio adaptation uses it as the coda of the final episode, spoken as written; no lines are cut or added.
