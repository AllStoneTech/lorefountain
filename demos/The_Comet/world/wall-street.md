Where Jim emerges from the bank's side door at "high-noon": "How silent the street was! Not a soul was stirring." Across from him, in a great stone doorway, a hundred men, women and children lie "crushed and twisted and jammed... like refuse in a can" in a rush to shelter. Nearby: a silk-hatted businessman standing "stone dead with wonder"; a woman at a signpost; a stopped street car; the newsboy in the gutter.
