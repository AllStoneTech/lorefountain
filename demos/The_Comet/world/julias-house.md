A great house near 72nd Street with an upper window, heavy door, silken hangings, mahogany and silver, a darkroom, and a garage in a court that holds "a swifter car," the Stutz. Inside lie a French maid and a butler dead. It is the first place Jim finds anyone living. The text puts the cry as he rushes "past 72nd Street" on Fifth Avenue; it does not say the house itself fronts on Fifth, so that parentage is an inference.
