[[Marrabo McSwayne]]'s son, a married man and an easy-going gentleman who dislikes trouble. In 1x02 his wife's illness takes [[Tenie]] away on the very day she and [[Sandy]] plan to run. In 1x03 he pays a card debt to [[Jeems McLean]] with a stray man he has just bought, then sells the same man to a trader when the overseer sends him back.
