A spring at the foot of the sand-hills, past the swamp, whose water is heavy with sulphur and iron and used by the household "for sanitary reasons." In 1x03 [[John]] and [[Annie]] wait there while it is cleaned out and watch a neighbor beat his horse. In 1x07 the short road to it is the road on which the mare balks.
