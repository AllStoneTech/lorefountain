The series' structural rule, taken straight from the book: every tale [[Uncle Julius McAdoo]] tells is, in the same breath, a plea, a pitch, a warning, or a cover story, and the closing act of every episode reveals what he got out of it. The twist in a series like this is not usually in the plot of the tale but in the last four minutes, when [[John]] realizes, nearly always correctly, that he has been had. Sometimes the old man's gain is petty (honey, a suit), sometimes it is human (a grandson's job, a wedding), and in 1x05 his gain and [[Annie]]'s are the same.
