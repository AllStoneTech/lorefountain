A field hand on [[Jeems McLean]]'s plantation whose sweetheart is sent off to the Robeson County plantation as a punishment. He carries a peck of corn to [[Aun' Peggy]], gives her powder to the cook for the master's soup, and later finds himself running errands for her by night, past the patrollers, to feed a conjured sweet potato to the one man who must not be shipped south. He is the only one who suspects what he has done.
