The supernatural craft that runs under every tale: roots, herbs, bottles, gourds, bags, dolls, hair, and a good deal of bargaining. A conjurer wants payment (corn, a ham, a pig, a handkerchief, a dollar), does only what she has promised, and can usually undo what she has done. Its rules in this series are the book's own: it transforms people into trees, mules, birds, cats, and wolves; it can be countered by another conjurer, but only if the right person is alive and in a condition to speak the language of the shape; and whites who say they do not believe in it, like [[Mars Dugal' McAdoo]], still leave a free conjure woman alone. The series never confirms that it is real. See [[Aun' Peggy]].
