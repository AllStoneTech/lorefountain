Bought by [[Marrabo McSwayne]] from a trader in the swap that cost [[Sandy]] his first wife, and who turns out to be a conjure woman, though she has done no conjuring for fifteen years or more since she "got religion." She breaks that vow for love, and the same plantation machinery that wore Sandy out then takes her away just when she is about to get them both free. She spends what is left of her life slipping round the haunted kitchen and then the schoolhouse at night, where she is found dead one winter morning.
