The liveliest hand on [[Jim McGee]]'s plantation, always dancing, drinking, and singing, who goes out at night against the rules and brings home a stranger's fat shote. [[The Guinea Conjurer]] turns him into a mule for it. Returned to human form at the last minute by a dying conjurer, he keeps a club foot from a cure that ran out of strength, and he will not tell anyone how he came by it.
