A good, capable hand owned by [[Marrabo McSwayne]], passed from one of his children to another every month until he hardly knows where he lives, then lent to a distant uncle's plantation. To stay near his new wife [[Tenie]], he asks her to turn him into a tree. The tree is cut down and sawed into planks, and the planks, in turn, carry his groan into a kitchen and a schoolhouse.
