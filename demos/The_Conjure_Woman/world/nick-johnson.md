[[Jeems McLean]]'s overseer, a poor white man, called "Ole Nick" behind his back by people who hate and despise him in equal parts. While his master is gone he cuts rations, lengthens tasks, and whips the stray man he cannot break. The master who returns asks him to tell the whole story in detail, praises him, and then fires him over a bluff about the account books; he is out of the neighborhood within two days.
