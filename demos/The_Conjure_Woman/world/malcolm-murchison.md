A young Southerner, proud and jealous of his honor, who breaks with [[Mabel]] and decides to leave for New York on the evening boat, probably never to return. He is walking down the road to catch his buggy when the rockaway of [[John]], [[Annie]] and Mabel makes its long detour. Afterward the book notes an excellent understanding between him and [[Uncle Julius McAdoo]], who had the chance to enter the young couple's service but chose to stay.
