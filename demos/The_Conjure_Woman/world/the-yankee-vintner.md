An unnamed Northern visitor who sells [[Mars Dugal' McAdoo]] a new winepress and a promise of twice the grapes. He has the vines dug around and dosed with a mixture of lime and ashes, and plays cards at the big house every evening while he does it. The vines burn up, and with them [[Henry]]. [[Uncle Julius McAdoo]] tells this part to the very Northerner who is deciding whether to buy the place.
