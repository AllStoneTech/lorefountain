[[Annie]]'s younger sister and [[John]]'s ward, high-spirited, jealous, and capable of terrible things said quickly. She breaks her engagement to [[Malcolm Murchison]] over his going to church with another girl, and spends ten days pretending she does not care. In 1x07 she is walked, without knowing it, toward a reconciliation that [[Uncle Julius McAdoo]] and [[Annie]] seem to have arranged.
