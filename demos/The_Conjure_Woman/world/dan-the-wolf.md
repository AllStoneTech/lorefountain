A strong, even-tempered man belonging to [[Mars Dugal' McAdoo]], who kills a free conjurer's son in a fight over [[Mahaly]], and then is tricked by the conjurer, [[Unk' Jube]], into killing Mahaly herself in the shape of a wolf. The spell that turned him is made permanent with the conjurer's last breath, and Dan lives and dies by Mahaly's grave. A different Dan from [[Dan the Plowman]].
