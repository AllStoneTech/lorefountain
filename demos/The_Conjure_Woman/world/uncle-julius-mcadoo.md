Born and raised on the McAdoo plantation, set free by the war, and still attached to the land that [[John]] has bought. He drives the couple's carriage, and he tells the tales: the goophered grapevine, the man turned to a tree, the master turned to a slave. Every tale carries a warning, a hint, or a request, and every request pays off for Julius in grapes, a schoolhouse, a grandson's job, a horse sale, honey, or a wedding. Whether he believes his own tales, invents them, or inherited them is left open on purpose.
