# The Conjure Woman

## Premise

[[John]] and [[Annie]] move from Ohio to North Carolina for Annie's health and buy the old McAdoo vineyard. [[Uncle Julius McAdoo]], who lives on the land, drives their carriage and tells them tales of slavery days in which conjure, most often [[Aun' Peggy]]'s, turns men into trees, mules, and wolves, and a hard master into a man who can be whipped. Each tale is an episode; each has a twist in its closing minutes, when John realizes what Julius gained from the telling.

## Setting

Central North Carolina some years after the Civil War, in the sand-hill country around a town called [[Patesville]]: pine barrens, swamp, a mineral spring, a sawmill, a plantation house ruined in the war. The tales themselves take place on neighboring plantations before the war.

## Key Characters

- **[[Uncle Julius McAdoo]]** — host, storyteller, schemer.
- **[[John]]** — the Northern skeptic.
- **[[Annie]]** — his wife, and Julius's best listener.
- **[[Aun' Peggy]]** — the conjure woman, mostly heard of, rarely seen.

## Synopsis

Seven self-contained half-hours: a goophered vineyard (1x01); a man turned into a tree and sawed into planks (1x02); a master who dreams himself into slavery (1x03); a man turned into a mule (1x04); a mother traded for a racehorse and her baby turned into a bird (1x05); a husband turned into a wolf who kills his own wife (1x06); and a house boy's burning feet that cost two lovers their lives (1x07). In the frame, Julius gets a grandson's job, a church meeting house, a horse sale, a private honey tree, and a wedding.

## Themes

Memory and the land; who owns a story; the way love is broken by sale and chance; reversal and revenge; and the double voice of a man who tells his employers what they want to hear while telling the audience something else.
