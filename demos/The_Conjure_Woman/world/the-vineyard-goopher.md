A big black bottle [[Aun' Peggy]] fills with a leaf, a grape hull, a seed, a twig, dirt, a snake's tooth, a speckled hen's gall, cat's hair, and scuppernong wine, then buries under a red oak. Anyone who eats the vines' grapes will die within twelve months. [[Henry]] eats them by mistake, and the remedy, rubbing the vines' own sap on his head each spring, binds him to the vine's seasons.
