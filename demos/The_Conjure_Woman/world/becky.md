A first-rate field hand whose husband was sold to Virginia when his owner's estate had to pay its debts. She is traded for a racehorse and carried off to a distant plantation to cook and wash, told she is going to Robeson County for a day or two. She feels the humming-bird and the mocking-bird, dreams of her baby, and then, after a conjured bag appears at her door, believes she herself has been conjured and begins to die.
