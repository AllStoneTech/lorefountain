The mill past the swamp where [[John]] goes to order lumber and where [[Uncle Julius McAdoo]], watching the up-and-down saw groan through a log, shudders and remembers [[Sandy]]. It is both the trigger and the setting of the climax of 1x02: [[Tenie]] is tied to a post and made to watch the tree sawed up.
