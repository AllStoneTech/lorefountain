The Black Baptist congregation that [[Uncle Julius McAdoo]] belongs to. It splits over temperance, and about half the members, Julius among them, secede and need a meeting place. They get [[The Old Schoolhouse]], and Julius reassures [[Annie]] that ghosts never disturb religious worship.
