A free Black conjure woman who lives near the Wilmington Road. [[Mars Dugal' McAdoo]] hires her to goopher his vines, and the enslaved come to her at night with whatever they can carry. She names her price, keeps track of her work so that it does no more than she intends, and is careful about conjuring white folks. Dugal' would have had her whipped long ago, Julius says, but she is a free woman and he is afraid she would conjure him. She appears in five of the seven tales.
