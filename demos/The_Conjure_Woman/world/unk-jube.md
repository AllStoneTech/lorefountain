The old free conjurer whose business [[Aun' Peggy]] has been eating into, and whose bad son is killed by [[Dan the Wolf]]. He traces the killer by his roots, tries a rattlesnake, poison, rheumatism, and fever, then breaks the life-charm, then befriends Dan, calls him the son he never had, and talks him into a hunt that ends with [[Mahaly]] dead. His show of fairness on his deathbed is a last trick; his dying breath finishes the curse.
