A doll made by [[Aun' Peggy]] with a corn-stalk body, splinter limbs, an elderberry-pith head, and two red peppers for feet, standing for [[Hannibal]]. Hidden under the doorsill of the big house, it makes him light-headed and hot-footed. [[Aun' Peggy]] told [[Jeff]] to bring it back when he was finished; in the rush of falling in love, nobody does, and Hannibal keeps stumbling for another month.
