A young hand on [[Jim McGee]]'s plantation who takes up with [[Sally]] while her husband is missing. He is plowing with the new mule when he tries to kiss her, and is flung into a cotton patch, kicked over a fence, and later terrified at his cabin window, never knowing that the mule is the husband he has just insulted.
