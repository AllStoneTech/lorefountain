A small raccoon-skin bag made by [[Aun' Peggy]] from two of [[Dan the Wolf]]'s hairs, a scrap of red flannel, and roots and herbs, to be buried in a bottle under a live oak. While it is safe, no poison, snake, or scorpion can kill him. [[Unk' Jube]] learns where it is from a jay-bird, wrecks the tree with wind, and washes the bottle down the river with rain.
