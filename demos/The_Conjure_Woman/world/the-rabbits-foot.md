A charm that [[Uncle Julius McAdoo]] says has to be the left hind foot of a graveyard rabbit, killed by a cross-eyed man on a dark night in the full of the moon. He says it has brought him forty years of good luck and he will lend it to someone he cares for but would never sell. By the end of the episode it has moved: a few weeks after the tale of [[Becky]], [[John]] is sent to fetch [[Annie]]'s handkerchief and finds the foot in her pocket.
