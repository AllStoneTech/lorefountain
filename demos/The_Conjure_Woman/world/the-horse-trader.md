A horse breeder who owns the champion racer Lightnin' Bug. He will not take a note, only a trade, and picks [[Becky]] out of [[Colonel Pendleton]]'s field hands. He refuses the baby as a bother and takes her away on a lie about Robeson County. When she sickens and the horse's knees swell, his conscience, he writes, will not let him sleep, and the trade is called off.
