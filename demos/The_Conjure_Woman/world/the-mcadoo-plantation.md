[[Mars Dugal' McAdoo]]'s plantation before the war, with its big house, quarters, smokehouse, winepress, and thousand gallons of scuppernong wine a year. It is where the tales of [[Henry]], [[Dan the Wolf]], and [[Chloe]] take place. The war destroyed the house, the heirs went to court, and the land lay neglected until [[John]] bought what is now [[The Vineyard]].
