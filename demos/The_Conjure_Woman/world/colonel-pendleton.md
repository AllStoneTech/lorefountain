A planter who loses money on his own horses, falls for a rival's champion racehorse named Lightnin' Bug, and trades [[Becky]], one of his best field hands, to get it. He cannot bring himself to take her baby away from her in person, so he lies and says she is only going to help his son-in-law for a day or two. When the horse's knees swell up, he threatens a lawsuit, then fears one, and finally accepts a swap back.
