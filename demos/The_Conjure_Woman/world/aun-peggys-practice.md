[[Aun' Peggy]]'s trade, seen in pieces. In 1x01 she is hired by a master to goopher his vines and later takes the goopher off a man who ate the grapes in ignorance, for a while. In 1x03 she gives Solomon the powder that, he suspects, turns a master into a man who can be whipped, and she keeps close track of the result. In 1x05 she haggles for peas and handkerchiefs. In 1x06 she is the good conjurer who is outmatched. In 1x07 she sells a doll. She is never seen on the vineyard, and never once in the frame.
