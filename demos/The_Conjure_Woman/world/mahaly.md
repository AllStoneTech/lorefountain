A tall, lively young woman on the McAdoo plantation, whom the whole quarter agrees is a good match for [[Dan the Wolf]]. A free conjurer's son pesters her. Later the conjurer himself lures her to his cabin on the pretext that her husband has been bitten by a spider, turns her into a black cat, and sets her loose where Dan is waiting. She is buried in the low ground, and her ghost is seen with a gray wolf.
