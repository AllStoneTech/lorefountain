The master of the McAdoo plantation and its scuppernong vineyard before the war. He hires [[Aun' Peggy]] to protect his grapes, then discovers that [[Henry]]'s goophered youth can be sold and resold; ruined by a visiting Yankee and then by the war, he goes off to fight, and the plantation never recovers. In the stories set on his land he is the man whose decisions the others pay for: [[Dan the Wolf]] and [[Chloe]] belong to him, and so does [[Jeff]], whom he sells south.
