The plantation nurse who minds [[Mose]] in the daytime. When he fails, she carries him with a small mess of green peas to [[Aun' Peggy]], haggles over the price, pays with a "monstrous small" mess, then a Sunday head-handkerchief, and does most of the leg-work of the cure. She wants Becky back for the sake of her own tired old bones, too.
