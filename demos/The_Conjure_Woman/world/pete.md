The man who tends [[Jim McGee]]'s mules. The dying conjurer sends for him to bring the new mule, and Pete rides it down by night, hands over the wrong bottle in the dark cabin, and holds the old man up while he works the last of the cure. He and [[Primus]] are the only two people in the cabin when the cure is done, and they carry the story back to the plantation together.
