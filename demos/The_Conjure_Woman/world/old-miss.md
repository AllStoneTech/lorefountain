[[Mars Dugal' McAdoo]]'s wife, who trains [[Chloe]] as her own maid. She is the one who speaks sharply when [[Hannibal]]'s burning feet wake her early, and the one who, once the doctor reports that Chloe is pining for [[Jeff]], tells her husband to buy him back. She says nothing to save Jeff in the first place, because she is still angry about her flower bulbs.
