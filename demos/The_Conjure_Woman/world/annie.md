[[John]]'s wife, whose poor health brought them South. She takes the plantation tales seriously where her husband does not, hearing in them, as she says after the first sawmill story, what a system it was that made such things possible. She dislikes a tale with no point, is moved by one with a true heart, and carries [[The Rabbit's Foot]] in her pocket by the end of 1x05.
