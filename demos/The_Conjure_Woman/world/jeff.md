He takes Chloe's silver dollar and silk handkerchief to [[Aun' Peggy]] and plants the doll under the doorsill, which gets him the house job and costs [[Hannibal]] his. When Chloe reports the doll, Dugal' sells him to a trader going down the river to Alabama; the trader reports that Jeff jumped or fell off the steamboat and drowned.
