[[Becky]]'s baby, who stops eating when his mother is taken away. [[Aun' Peggy]] turns him into a humming-bird and a mocking-bird in turn to fly to the distant plantation and see her. He grows up to sing and whistle for the white family at night, learn blacksmithing, hire his own time from [[Colonel Pendleton]], buy his mother's freedom first, and then his own.
