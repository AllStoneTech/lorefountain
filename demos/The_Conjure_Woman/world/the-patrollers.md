The mounted night patrols that ride the roads to keep the enslaved from visiting between plantations. [[Primus]] cuts across the cotton fields to avoid them; [[Solomon]], carrying [[Aun' Peggy]]'s sprinkled mixture, walks past them unseen. They are the nearest the world of the tales has to a state.
