A hard master with a big plantation, who forbids singing, courting, and marriage on his land and sells or exiles anyone who falls in love. Slipped a conjured powder in his okra soup, he announces he will be away a month and rides off, and about three or four days later a stray man appears at the plantation, with no memory and no name. When he returns, ashen and barefoot in a poor white man's clothes, he is a changed master, and the story says, by the lips of [[Solomon]], that the two were always the same man.
