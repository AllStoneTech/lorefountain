A spry older man bought by [[Mars Dugal' McAdoo]] to replace a dead field hand, who eats the goophered scuppernongs by mistake and is saved by [[Aun' Peggy]], at the price of rubbing the vines' sap on his head each spring. His hair, strength, and joints now follow the vines, which Dugal' turns into a business: sell him in spring, buy him back in the fall. When a Yankee's improvements kill the big vine, Henry dies with it.
