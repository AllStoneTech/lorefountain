When the sap rises and [[Henry]] gets young, [[Mars Dugal' McAdoo]] sells him, the first time for fifteen hundred dollars; when the sap drops and he sickens, Dugal' buys him back, the first time for five hundred. The buyers never learn about the goopher. The profit pays for another plantation on Beaver Creek.
