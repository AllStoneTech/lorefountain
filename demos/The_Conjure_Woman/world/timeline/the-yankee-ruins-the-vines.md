A visiting Yankee persuades [[Mars Dugal' McAdoo]] to dig up the vines and dose them with lime and ashes, and in a week of card games ruins them. As the vines die, [[Henry]] sickens; when the big vine from which he took his sap withers, he dies. The war then ends the plantation.
