[[Jeff]] brings [[Aun' Peggy]] a silver dollar and a handkerchief from [[Chloe]] and plants a doll under the big-house sill. [[Hannibal]]'s feet burn and his wits scramble; he is sent back to the corn fields, and Jeff takes the house job. Hannibal tells Chloe that Jeff is meeting another woman, shows her, in a sunbonnet, and she tells [[Mars Dugal' McAdoo]] about the doll. Dugal' sells Jeff to a trader bound for Alabama.
