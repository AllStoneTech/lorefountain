Lent to a kinsman's plantation forty miles away, [[Sandy]] begs [[Tenie]] to make him something that can stay put. She turns him into a pine at the edge of the swamp, and turns him back at night until a woodpecker, a turpentine box, and a day when Tenie is called away leave him with no one to watch the tree.
