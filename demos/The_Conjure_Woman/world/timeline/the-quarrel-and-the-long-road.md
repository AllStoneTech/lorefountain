[[John]] overhears [[Mabel]] and [[Malcolm Murchison]] break their engagement. Ten days later, [[Annie]] insists on the short road, the mare balks at the branch, and [[Uncle Julius McAdoo]] tells [[Chloe]]'s story. On the long road home they pass Malcolm's servant with his trunk, send Mabel ahead on foot, and Julius takes a long time finding Annie's fan; the next thing John sees is the two of them walking arm in arm.
