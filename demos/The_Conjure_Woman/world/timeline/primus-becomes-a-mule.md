[[Primus]] walks home from a dance and finds himself carrying a stranger's fat shote, which belongs to [[The Guinea Conjurer]]. The conjurer turns him into a mule and has a poor white man sell the mule to [[Jim McGee]]. The mule gets drunk on sharp wine at the McAdoo winepress, flings his wife's new sweetheart [[Dan the Plowman]] into a cotton patch, and kicks him over a fence.
