[[John]] says he will buy a mule to plow his watermelon patch; [[Uncle Julius McAdoo]] tells him about [[Primus]]. [[Annie]] calls the tale pointless. Julius adds that he knows a man with a good horse, and the horse turns out to be blind in one eye, broken-winded, and dead in a few months. Julius turns up the next Sunday in a new store suit.
