On a rainy afternoon, [[John]] reads a volume of philosophy to [[Annie]] until [[Uncle Julius McAdoo]] arrives under his umbrella and warns him against clearing [[The Low Ground]]. John clears it the next year, finds no grave and no bones, and a bee tree that, he suspects, Julius has been robbing for years.
