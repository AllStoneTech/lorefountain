While [[Tenie]] is nursing [[Dunkin McSwayne]]'s wife twenty miles away, [[Marrabo McSwayne]] fells the nearest big pine for a new kitchen and sends it to the mill. Tenie returns the next morning and throws herself on the log before the saw, but she is tied to a post and made to watch it cut up, while the log groans the whole way.
