[[Aun' Peggy]] turns [[Mose]] into a humming-bird and a mocking-bird to visit his mother, then sends a hornet to sting the racehorse's knees and a sparrow to watch the household. She drops a conjure bag at [[Becky]]'s door; Becky takes to her bed to die, the trader decides a lame horse is better than a dead woman, and the trade is undone.
