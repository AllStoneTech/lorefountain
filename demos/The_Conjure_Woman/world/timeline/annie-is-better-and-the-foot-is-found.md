At the end of their second year, [[Annie]] falls into a settled melancholy. [[Uncle Julius McAdoo]] comes up with [[The Rabbit's Foot]] and the tale of [[Becky]], which lifts her spirits that afternoon. A few weeks later, sent to fetch her handkerchief, [[John]] finds the rabbit's foot in her pocket.
