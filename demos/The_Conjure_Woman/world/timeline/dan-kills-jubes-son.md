A free man, the son of the conjurer [[Unk' Jube]], keeps following [[Mahaly]] on the road. [[Dan the Wolf]] confronts him, the man pulls a knife, and Dan hits him once, hard enough that he does not get up. No one sees; nothing is done; Jube buries his son, and starts working his roots to find the killer.
