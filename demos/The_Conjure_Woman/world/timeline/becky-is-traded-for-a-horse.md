[[Colonel Pendleton]] wants [[The Horse Trader]]'s racer Lightnin' Bug. The trader will not take a note and will not take the baby, only [[Becky]]. The colonel tells her she is going to Robeson County for a day or two and sends her off in the trader's buggy without [[Mose]]. A few miles down the Lumberton road the trader turns the buggy the wrong way, and she learns where she is really going.
