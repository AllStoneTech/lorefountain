Back home, [[Jeems McLean]] makes [[Nick Johnson]] describe, with relish, everything done to the stray man, then dismisses him on a bluff about the accounts. He shortens the tasks, allows dances, brings [[Solomon]]'s sweetheart home, and eight or ten couples marry. [[Libbie McSwayne]] hears of it, takes him back, and there is a wedding.
