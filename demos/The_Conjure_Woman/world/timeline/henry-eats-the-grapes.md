[[Henry]], new on the plantation and unwarned, eats the goophered scuppernongs while [[Mars Dugal' McAdoo]] and the neighbors are out after a runaway and no one thinks to warn him. [[Aun' Peggy]] takes the goopher off him until spring; then, for a ham, she ties his body to the vines' seasons: spry and curly-haired in spring and summer, bald and stiff in fall and winter.
