After his snakes, poisons, and rheumatism fail against [[The Life-Charm]], [[Unk' Jube]] washes the charm into the river, rides Dan nightly, and then offers to turn him into a wolf to kill the witch who has been riding him. He lures [[Mahaly]] to his cabin and turns her into a black cat. [[Dan the Wolf]] tears out her throat, goes back and kills Jube, and takes the dying man's cure, which is the final trick: "Wolf you is en wolf you stays."
