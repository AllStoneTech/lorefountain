[[John]] and [[Annie]] drive out from [[Patesville]] to look at the ruined McAdoo place. Under an elm they find [[Uncle Julius McAdoo]] eating scuppernongs, and he advises them not to buy, because the vineyard is goophered. They buy it anyway, and keep him on as coachman.
