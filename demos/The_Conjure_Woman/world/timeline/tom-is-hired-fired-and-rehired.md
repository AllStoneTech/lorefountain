[[John]] hires [[Tom]] to please [[Uncle Julius McAdoo]], fires him for laziness, then drives with [[Annie]] to [[The Mineral Spring]], where Julius tells the tale of [[Jeems McLean]]. The next evening, after a day in town and at a neighbor's, John finds Tom carrying water to the barn, reinstated by Annie after he hung round the place looking "down in the mouth."
