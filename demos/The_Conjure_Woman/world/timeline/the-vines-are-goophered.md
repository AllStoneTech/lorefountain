Missing scuppernongs and a leg full of cow-peas from his own spring gun drive [[Mars Dugal' McAdoo]] to hire [[Aun' Peggy]] to goopher the vines, for ten dollars, Julius reckons. A coachman and a child die after eating the grapes, and the quarter leaves the vines alone. That season's wine pays him back fifteen hundred gallons on a ten-dollar outlay.
