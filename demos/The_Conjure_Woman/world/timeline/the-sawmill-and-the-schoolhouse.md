At the sawmill, the saw's moaning sets [[Uncle Julius McAdoo]] off on the story of [[Sandy]]. [[Annie]] decides she does not want her new kitchen built of that lumber, and [[John]] buys new boards instead. Soon afterward Julius, one of the seceders from the Sandy Run church, asks to hold their meetings in [[The Old Schoolhouse]] and gets it.
