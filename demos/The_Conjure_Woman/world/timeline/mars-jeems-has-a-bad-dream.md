[[Solomon]] gets a powder from [[Aun' Peggy]] and has it put in [[Jeems McLean]]'s okra soup. The master announces he will be gone a month, and a few days later a stray man arrives who can't remember his name, is whipped and starved by the overseer [[Nick Johnson]], and is sold to a trader. When the trader's gang is about to ship out, Solomon slips a conjured sweet potato to the man in the dark. The next day, a Sunday, Jeems is standing in the woods, pale and barefoot, in a poor white man's clothes.
