After a camp-meeting conversion and a bad cold from the mourners' bench, [[The Guinea Conjurer]] sends for the mule via [[Pete]], and works his roots to turn [[Primus]] back. Pete gives him the wrong bottle in the dark, the old man poisons himself, and he dies holding on just long enough to turn Primus back, all but one foot.
