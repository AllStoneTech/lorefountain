Hannibal laughs and tells [[Chloe]] what he did. She faints, sickens, and pines. [[Old Miss]] has her husband write to buy [[Jeff]] back; the trader replies that Jeff went overboard on the steamboat and drowned. Chloe takes to sitting under a willow by [[The Willow Branch]], waiting, and one night is found drowned in the creek.
