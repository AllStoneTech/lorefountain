A planter on the far side of the swamp, next to what became the vineyard. He lends [[Sandy]] to his married children in rotation to avoid choosing among them, swaps Sandy's first wife away to a trader, and cuts down a convenient pine for a new kitchen without any idea what it is. When the kitchen is haunted he tears it down and builds the old schoolhouse from its boards, then sells the land to [[Mars Dugal' McAdoo]]. His eldest daughter is [[Libbie McSwayne]].
