A small one-room pine house on the northeast corner of the vineyard, with a big crumbling brick chimney and a shutter fallen from its hinge. It was built, Julius says, from the boards of [[Marrabo McSwayne]]'s haunted kitchen, which were sawed from the tree that was [[Sandy]]. [[John]] wants to tear it down for a new kitchen; [[Annie]] changes her mind, and in the end the seceders from the Sandy Run church get to meet in it.
