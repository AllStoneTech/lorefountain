[[Old Miss]]'s trained maid, promised by [[Mars Dugal' McAdoo]] to the new house boy [[Hannibal]] as a wife. She wants [[Jeff]] instead, arranges to have Hannibal conjured out of the house, and then, misled into jealousy, tells the master everything and gets Jeff sold south. When she learns the truth, she pines, wanders to the willow tree on the far side of a branch, waits for him night after night, and is found drowned in the water. Julius says her ghost still sits there.
