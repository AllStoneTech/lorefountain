A neck of woods down by the swamp that [[John]] wants to clear: no good for grapes, too low for cotton, in danger of raccoons for corn. [[Uncle Julius McAdoo]] says [[Mahaly]] is buried there and that [[Dan the Wolf]] howls over her grave. Cleared a year later, it shows no grave, no bones, and a bee tree.
