The thread under all six antebellum tales after the first: couples and families that the enslaved were not allowed to keep. [[Sandy]] and [[Tenie]] are separated by lending and swapping; [[Solomon]]'s sweetheart is sent off to another plantation; [[Primus]]'s wife takes another man; [[Becky]] is traded away from her baby; [[Mahaly]] is murdered by the man who loves her; [[Chloe]] loses [[Jeff]] to the river. The frame's own pair, [[Mabel]] and [[Malcolm Murchison]], are the only lovers who get a happy ending.
