The ruined McAdoo estate that [[John]] buys: decayed gateposts, a straight sandy lane, rotting rail fence, the standing chimneys of a mansion lost to the war, and a wild, unpruned vineyard where [[Uncle Julius McAdoo]] eats grapes under an elm. It is where most frame scenes take place, and the series' "home base": a piazza, a lane, a drive down to the plank-road, and a rocking-chair for Julius that is never quite his.
