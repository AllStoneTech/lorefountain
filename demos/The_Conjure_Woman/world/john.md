A Northern grape-grower from Ohio who moves to central North Carolina for his wife's health and buys the ruined McAdoo vineyard. He keeps [[Uncle Julius McAdoo]] on as coachman, listens to every tale with polite disbelief, and spends each episode's last act working out what the old man was really after. His skepticism is the audience's way in; the series' joke is that he is usually right about the motive and wrong about everything else.
