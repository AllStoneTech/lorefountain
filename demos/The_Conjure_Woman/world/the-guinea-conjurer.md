A free conjurer on the Wilmington Road who says he was born in Africa, the son of a king or governor. His shote is stolen by [[Primus]], so he turns the thief into a mule and sells the mule back to Primus's own master through a poor white man. A camp-meeting conversion changes his heart, and from a sick bed he sends for the mule and tries to undo it, only to drink poison from the wrong bottle before he finishes the last foot.
