[[Uncle Julius McAdoo]]'s seventeen-year-old grandson, hired by [[John]] to help with the stables, garden, and wood, mostly to please the old man. He is lazy, careless, and untrustworthy, and is fired in the first act of 1x03. By the last act [[Annie]] has given him another chance, which is the whole point of the story Julius tells in between.
