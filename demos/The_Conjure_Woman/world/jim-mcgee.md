A planter on the Lumberton plank-road who advertises for his runaway [[Primus]], then buys a handsome mule for fifty dollars from a poor white man near the Wilmington Road and has no idea whom he is plowing with. When Primus comes back with a crooked foot and a story, he doesn't tie him up; the enslaved decide he must have had his own suspicions about that conjurer.
