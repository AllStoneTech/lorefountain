# Period language, dialect, and a broadcast version

The scripts keep the book's racial slurs and heavy dialect in the characters' dialogue, as a faithful adaptation of a historical text. A real production would need a decision about how much dialect to put in actors' mouths, who should write and consult on it, and whether any broadcast cut changes the language. The adaptation also cuts the book's narrator's racial commentary; Julius is the host.

Also open: running time. Each script is a condensed treatment of a long tale; a true half-hour will need further cuts, especially 1x03, 1x05, and 1x06.

Not yet adapted: the three uncollected Julius stories listed in the book's appendix ("Dave's Neckliss," "A Deep Sleeper," "Lonesome Ben") — natural material for a second season.
