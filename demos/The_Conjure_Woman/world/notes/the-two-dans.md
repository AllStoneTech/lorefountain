# The two Dans, and other name collisions

The book uses "Dan" for two unrelated men: the plowman in "The Conjurer's Revenge" and the man turned into a wolf in "The Gray Wolf's Ha'nt." There are two Marrabos (McSwayne in 1x02/1x03, Utley in 1x07), two Lucys (the cook in 1x01, the mare in 1x07), and "Mars Jeems" in Sandy's lament may or may not be McLean.

The book also contradicts itself in places the adaptation keeps rather than smooths over. [[Uncle Julius McAdoo]] says he lives "behine de nex' san'-hill, on de Lumberton plank-road," yet John finds he has long occupied a cabin on the vineyard itself. [[Aun' Peggy]] is the established cunjuh 'oman of 1x01 and 1x03, but in 1x06 she is the newcomer who has "sot up in de bizness" against old [[Unk' Jube]].
Open question: do we rename one Dan on screen, or keep the collision as a wink that Julius reuses names? The entities are currently `Dan the Plowman` and `Dan the Wolf`, with no shared alias, so scripts don't auto-link a bare "Dan."
