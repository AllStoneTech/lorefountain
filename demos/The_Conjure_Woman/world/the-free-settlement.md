The settlement of free Black people and poor whites along the Wilmington Road, where [[Aun' Peggy]] and [[The Guinea Conjurer]] each keep a cabin and a trade, and where [[Primus]] goes dancing. The enslaved of the neighboring plantations are afraid of the conjurers who live there, and go there at night when they need help.
