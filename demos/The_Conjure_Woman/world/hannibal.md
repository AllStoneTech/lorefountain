The quick, vain house boy that [[Mars Dugal' McAdoo]] prefers to [[Jeff]]. When [[Aun' Peggy]]'s doll sets his feet on fire and scrambles his wits, he breaks dishes, feeds the mistress's flower bulbs to the hogs, and is sent back to the corn fields. He gets his revenge by dressing as a woman in a sunbonnet for [[Jeff]] to run to, so that [[Chloe]], watching, will believe him unfaithful, and then he laughs in her face about it.
