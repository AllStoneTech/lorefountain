An amber-colored creek crossing the short road through the swamp toward the mineral spring, with a bay tree in blossom on one side and a drooping willow on the other. [[Chloe]] drowned in it. A gray mare, [[Uncle Julius McAdoo]] says, can see a ghost that walks on a Friday, and that is why this one balks.
