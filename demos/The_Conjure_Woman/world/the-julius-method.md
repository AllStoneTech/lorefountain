The running comedy, and the season's real plot: each week [[Uncle Julius McAdoo]] gets something, and each week [[John]] works out how. The ledger: the vineyard's grapes (1x01), a church meeting place (1x02), his grandson's job (1x03), a suspected commission on a bad horse and a new suit (1x04), nothing he will admit to, only [[Annie]]'s recovery (1x05), a private supply of honey (1x06), a reconciliation he may have rigged with a young man who then offers him a job (1x07).
