---
term: Goopher
gloss: A conjurer's charm or spell, and the verb for casting one ("to goopher" something).
aliases: [goophered, goophering, goopher mixtry, cunj'in']
tags: [conjure, dialect]
---

The word that opens the book: "Is goophered,--cunju'd, bewitch'." A goophered vine, a goophered sweet potato, a goopher mixture. Used by [[Uncle Julius McAdoo]] as both noun and verb, and glossed for the narrator on first use.
