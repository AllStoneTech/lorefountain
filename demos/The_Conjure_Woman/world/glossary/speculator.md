---
term: Speculator
gloss: A slave trader who bought people in one place to resell them at profit elsewhere, usually farther South.
aliases: [spekilater, spekilaters, speculators, slave trader, trader]
tags: [dialect, slavery, period-term]
---

Traders turn up in three tales: the man who swaps [[Tenie]] for [[Sandy]]'s first wife, the trader who buys the stray man in [[Jeems McLean]]'s story and ships him toward New Orleans, and the buyer who takes [[Jeff]] down the river toward Alabama. The speculator is the series' off-screen engine of loss.
