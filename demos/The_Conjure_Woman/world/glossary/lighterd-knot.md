---
term: Lighterd-knot
gloss: A resin-rich knot of pine wood, burned for torchlight or used as a club.
aliases: [lighter knot, fat pine, lighterd knot]
tags: [dialect, setting]
---

The conjurer tells [[Dan the Wolf]] to hit the witch "in de head wid a rock er a lighterd-knot." It is also the torch flickering on the hearth when Dan comes back to kill [[Unk' Jube]].
