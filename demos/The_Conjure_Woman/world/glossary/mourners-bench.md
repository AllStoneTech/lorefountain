---
term: Mourners' bench
gloss: The front bench of a revival meeting where those seeking conversion knelt to pray and "mourn" for their sins.
aliases: [mourner's bench, camp meeting, camp-meeting]
tags: [religion, period-term]
---

The [[The Guinea Conjurer]] gets his fatal cold kneeling at the mourners' bench during a camp meeting on the Wilmington Road, the same meeting that turns him toward undoing his work.
