term: Free Negro
gloss: Period legal term for a Black person who was not enslaved; such people lived under severe restrictions in antebellum North Carolina.

[[Aun' Peggy]] is one; so is [[The Guinea Conjurer]], freed by a grateful master, and so was [[Unk' Jube]]'s son. Free status is also what protects a conjurer from a planter's whip, as [[Mars Dugal' McAdoo]] knows.
