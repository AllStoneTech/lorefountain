---
term: Scuppernong
gloss: A sweet native Southern grape of the muscadine family, thick-skinned and golden-bronze when ripe.
aliases: [scuppernon', scuppernons, scuppernongs, scuppernon' vimes]
tags: [grape, setting]
---

The grape [[John]] plans to build his business on, and the one [[Mars Dugal' McAdoo]] made wine from. Julius's loving description of it, "sugar ain't a suckumstance ter scuppernon'," is the first poetry in the book.
