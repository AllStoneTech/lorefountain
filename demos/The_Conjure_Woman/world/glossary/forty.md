---
term: Forty
gloss: Forty lashes, the standard whipping punishment on the plantations of the tales.
aliases: [fo'ty, forty lashes, gun 'im fo'ty]
tags: [slavery, violence, period-term]
---

"Gun 'im fo'ty" means "gave him forty lashes." Overseers in the book hand out forty as a matter of course; [[Jeems McLean]] receives several rounds of them, in his tale, without a name.
