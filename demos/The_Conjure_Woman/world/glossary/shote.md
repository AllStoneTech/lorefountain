---
term: Shote
gloss: A young hog, weaned and fattening, not yet full grown.
aliases: [shoat, shotes]
tags: [dialect, livestock]
---

The stolen pig that gets [[Primus]] turned into a mule; and, in a smaller way, the pig [[Dan the Wolf]] carries to [[Aun' Peggy]]'s cabin as payment for his life-charm.
