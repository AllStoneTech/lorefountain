---
term: Buckrah
gloss: A white man, here usually a poor one; period dialect of Black Southern speech, from a word for "white man."
aliases: [po' buckrah, buckrahs, po' w'ite man, po' white man]
tags: [dialect, class]
---

In the tales the word often carries class contempt as well as race: "a po' buckrah" is a white man with no property of his own. [[Nick Johnson]] is the standard example, and the enslaved despise him precisely because he owns nobody.
