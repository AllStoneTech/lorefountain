---
term: Cunjuh
gloss: Dialect form of "conjure" and "conjurer"; a cunjuh man or cunjuh 'oman is a person who works roots and charms.
aliases: [cunjuh man, cunjuh 'oman, cunjuh woman, conjure man, conjure woman, conjurer, conjuh]
tags: [conjure, dialect]
---

The spoken form of the conjurer's trade. "Conjure woman" in the book's title is the standard-English form of what [[Uncle Julius McAdoo]] calls a cunjuh 'oman.
