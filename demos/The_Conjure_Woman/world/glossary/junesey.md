---
term: Junesey
gloss: Dialect for a sweetheart; "juneseyin'" is courting.
aliases: [juneseyin', junesey-in]
tags: [dialect]
---

The Chesnutt text footnotes it simply as "Sweetheart." In [[Jeems McLean]]'s tale the master forbids courting on his plantation; in the book's footnote the word is glossed for the Northern reader.
