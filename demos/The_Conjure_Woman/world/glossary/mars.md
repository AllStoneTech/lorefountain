---
term: Mars
gloss: Dialect form of "Master," used before a slaveholder's name ("Mars Dugal'"); "ole mis'" and "young missis" are the matching forms for white women.
aliases: [marster, Mars', mistiss, mis']
tags: [dialect, address]
---

The honorific in front of nearly every white name in the tales. Julius uses "Marster" for the old owner and still says "Mis' Annie" for the new mistress, in the same register.
