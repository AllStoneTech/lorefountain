---
term: Ha'nt
gloss: A ghost or haunt; Southern dialect for a restless spirit that keeps to the place of its wrong.
aliases: [ha'nts, haunt, haunts, haunted, ha'nted, sperrit]
tags: [dialect, ghost]
---

Julius's ghosts are always tied to a place: a kitchen, a schoolhouse, a creek, a low piece of ground. They are warnings, and also real-estate advice.
