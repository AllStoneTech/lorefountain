# Previously, on Loomwake

*This is a player-safe recap, current as of the end of session `1x05`. It's hand-built — LoreFountain doesn't yet have a real "export as-of a production code" command; this is what that command would produce if it existed, assembled by hand by cross-checking every entity and relation against what's actually been played, and leaving out anything still marked as unrevealed (a `production_code` later than `1x05`, or a relation scoped to `participants` that isn't the whole table). Six weeks ago, every ship, station, and settlement in the Sable Verge picked up the same signal at once — eleven seconds, on every channel, and nobody could say if it was a voice, code, or something else entirely. It traced back to a structure the size of a small moon that most people weren't sure was even real: the Cael Loom, dark and silent for longer than anyone's kept records, until it wasn't.

Your ship, the *Loose Thread*, got there first — ahead of the corporations, the zealots, and everyone else now racing to the same coordinates.

**In the debris outside the Loom**, you found something: a fist-sized fragment, warm, inscribed with the same pattern as the signal itself, that hums whenever the Loom does. Before you'd even decided what to do with it, an unregistered Vhel vessel made contact — an elder who called herself Mev-Kesh, who didn't explain much, only warned you that everyone converging on the Loom had already heard the same signal you had, and that you should decide what to do with what you'd found before they arrived.

**At Halyard Station**, the Tessera Concern — a salvage corporation that filed an exclusive license on the Loom suspiciously fast — offered you a contract: scout the Loom's hull quietly, first look at anything you find in exchange for their protection and payment. Their fixer, a sharp, likable operator who goes by Auger, has been genuinely useful so far.

**The Kindled Choir**, a militant faith camped at the far end of the promenade, reads the signal as the start of something they call the Unveiling. Their field commander, Choir-Marshal Yevet Sarn, recognized the pattern on your fragment immediately — she says it's a countdown, translated off the Loom's own inscriptions. Whether she's right isn't confirmed. She's not obviously wrong, either.

**In open space near Halyard**, a Hollow Chain crew under Captain Dray Ushant tried to take the fragment from you — by offer, then by threat. You kept it. Whatever happened out there, word has spread: every faction at the Loom either knows you're holding something valuable, or will soon.

**Most recently**, Commodore Iyoha Sten of the Verge Compact — running three peacekeeping ships built for a fraction of the traffic now converging on the Loom — pulled you aside for an honest conversation about what's actually going on. Mid-conversation, every console alarmed at once: the Loom itself is changing. A seam that wasn't there this morning has split open along the outer ring, and every faction's ships are moving toward it at once.

Nobody agreed on that. It just started happening.

That's where it stands.
