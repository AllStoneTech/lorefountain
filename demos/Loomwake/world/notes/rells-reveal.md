# How does Rell's secret actually come out?

Jordan (Rell's player) should get to choose the moment, not have it forced by the plot — that's the whole point of a `participants`-scoped secret being player-owned. Options to offer Jordan when the time feels right:
- Rell chooses to reveal it to the crew directly, on their own terms.
- Something at the Loom (a Vhel-specific reaction, a scan, Mev-Kesh recognizing them) forces it into the open.
- Rell tells one other crew member first, privately, before the whole table knows — which would need its own `participants` list, not a jump straight to table-wide.

Don't default to the third option just because it's the most "dramatic" — ask Jordan first.
