# Does the Compact actually get reinforcements?

Sten's been operating on three ships the whole campaign. Whether the wider Compact government sends more once the Loom starts visibly changing (1x05's cliffhanger) is open — could go either way:
- Reinforcements arrive, and the Compact suddenly has real leverage it didn't have before.
- Nothing comes, because the wider Compact government is slower and more political than Sten is, and that gap becomes its own source of tension with her.

The second option is more interesting and probably truer to how under-resourced she's been played so far.
