Ship's medic and the *Loose Thread*'s resident mystic, an [[Ilvahn]] whose people communicate as much through scent and stillness as speech. Thistle heard something in the Loomwake signal none of the instruments picked up, and hasn't decided how much of that to tell the rest of the crew.
