A loosely settled frontier region on nobody's core shipping lanes — which is exactly why the Cael Loom sat undisturbed here for as long as it did. Home to independent traders, the Verge Compact's threadbare peacekeeping presence, and now, since the signal, considerably more traffic than it's built for.
