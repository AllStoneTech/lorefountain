Four factions circling the same prize with incompatible goals, none of them strong enough to simply take it and be done. The party's actual leverage across this campaign comes from being the one party at the Loom none of the four fully control — shares episodes with [[The Loom's Reckoning]] on purpose, since the mystery and the politics keep colliding.
