An elder of the [[Vhel]] diaspora, and unlike [[Auger]], openly and unmistakably Vhel to anyone who meets her — the contrast is deliberate. Mev-Kesh has spent decades collecting the diaspora's oldest, most contradictory stories about the home they lost, and she's the party's first real lead into what the Loom actually is.
