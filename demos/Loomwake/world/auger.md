Auger's actual name is Ashvane, and Auger is [[Vhel]], embedded in Tessera under a human shape for reasons that have nothing to do with the Concern's paycheck. The relation above is deliberately marked `production_code: "1x09"` — a session past the end of what's currently written — so this project's own material demonstrates a secret still genuinely unrevealed at the point the campaign, as written, currently stops.
