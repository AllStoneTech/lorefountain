Whoever built [[the Cael Loom]] and then vanished so completely that not even their name survived anywhere but the structure itself — "the Cael" is a label the Kindled Choir's translators coined, not a name anyone's confirmed the builders used for themselves. Marked `tentative` deliberately: almost everything believed about them, including their relationship to the [[Vhel]], is inference from a six-week-old signal and a handful of inscriptions, not established fact.
