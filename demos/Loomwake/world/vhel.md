A spacefaring people capable of consciously reshaping their own form and voice over time — not an instant disguise, a real, gradual physical change, referred to among themselves as [[Reflowing]]. Scattered across the Verge and beyond for longer than anyone's kept records, with no unified homeland and, per this project's `world/OVERVIEW.md`, no single faction speaking for all of them: some, like [[Mev-Kesh]], live openly as Vhel; others, like [[Auger]], don't, for reasons the campaign treats as genuinely their own rather than a species-wide trait. Whether they descend from [[the Cael]] is the mystery the whole campaign is built around, not a settled fact.
