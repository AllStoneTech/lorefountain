A fist-sized fragment of dense, faintly warm material [[Nine]] pulled out of debris drifting near the Loom's outer hull, inscribed with the same eleven-tone pattern as the Loomwake signal itself. It hums, faintly and irregularly, whenever the Loom's broadcast changes — the party's only lead that isn't controlled by a faction, and every faction at the Loom would very much like to know they have it.
