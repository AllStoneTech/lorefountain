# Session Zero — Loomwake

A short conversation to have with your table before `1x01`, not a rulebook.

## Tone

Pulpy, fast, competent-crew space opera — closer to a heist crew caught between bigger players than a grim survival story. Real stakes, but banter doesn't stop the plot from moving. See `world/OVERVIEW.md` for the fuller pitch, or hand players `PREVIOUSLY-ON.md` once the campaign is underway.

## Where this gets uncomfortable, on purpose

This campaign's core mechanic is a secret about identity and trust — someone close to the party isn't what they appear to be, and it's played straight rather than as a gotcha. Worth naming to your table before session one, plainly:

- Nobody's asked to keep an out-of-game secret from another player except where a `relations[]` entry is explicitly scoped to one seat at the table (`participants`) — check `world/README.md`'s note on this before assuming a secret is yours to share or keep.
- If "an ally isn't who they seem" is a theme that's uncomfortable for anyone at your table for real reasons — not just "I don't like being surprised" — better to say so now than mid-scene.

## A few quick questions worth asking your table

- Comfortable with combat having real stakes (character injury, hard choices under pressure)? This campaign doesn't shy from consequences, but nothing here is written to be gratuitous.
- Any lines around body horror or identity-based deception specifically, given the Vhel are central to the plot? Doesn't need to be a long conversation — just enough that nobody's blindsided.
- Who's tracking what the party currently knows vs. what's still secret? Recommend the GM keep `world/notes/` open during prep, and treat anything in `world/` marked with a `production_code` later than the current session, or `participants` that doesn't include everyone, as genuinely off-limits to say out loud at the table.

That's it — this doesn't need to be heavier than the game itself is.
