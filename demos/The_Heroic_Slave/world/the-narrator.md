The authorial voice that opens the novella, mourning that Madison "lives now only in the chattel records of his native State", and which keeps interrupting the story with judgments ("Here was a scene for the pencil!"). In the audio drama the narrator also frames each episode and delivers Part 4's opening indictment of the American slave trade.
