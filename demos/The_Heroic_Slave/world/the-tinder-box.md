An old-fashioned tinder-box that Madison pulls from his bundle, striking a piece of file on a flint to bring "a dozen sparks at once". "It is the only property saved from the fire in the dismal swamp." He has had it more than five years and has "broiled many a chicken" with it. A beautiful sound cue for 1x02 and, by echo, for the files in 1x05.
