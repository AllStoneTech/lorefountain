A dark pine forest near a sparkling brook in Virginia, within hearing of a distant village's church bells. Here, on a Sabbath morning in spring 1835, [[Madison Washington]] speaks alone and [[Mr. Listwell]] listens from behind a fallen tree; later it is where [[Susan]] meets Madison at midnight and then weekly for five years.
