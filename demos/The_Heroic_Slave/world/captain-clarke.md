Captain of the [[The Creole]]. He is the "imperious voice" who sets the brig under sail at Richmond, and the man pacing the starboard quarter-deck, armed, when the rising begins. He lies dying on the deck when Madison takes the helm, and his body is washed overboard by the first sea of the squall.
