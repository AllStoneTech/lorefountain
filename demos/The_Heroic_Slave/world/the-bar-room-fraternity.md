The half-dozen idlers, gamblers and "holders-on to the slack" who live in the tavern bar-room. They bet on who Listwell is, drink whiskey and tell stories of fights and duels with a relish the narrator declines to repeat. Useful as a chorus for the room below Listwell's floorboards.
