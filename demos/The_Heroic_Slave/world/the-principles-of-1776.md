The comparison that opens and closes the novella: Madison "loved liberty as well as did Patrick Henry... deserved it as much as Thomas Jefferson". At the helm he says, "We have done that which you applaud your fathers for doing, and if we are murderers, so were they." Grant, afterwards: "it was not that his principles were wrong in the abstract; for they are the principles of 1776. But I could not bring myself to recognize their application to one whom I deemed my inferior."
