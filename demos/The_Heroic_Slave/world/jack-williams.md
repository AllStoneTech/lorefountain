The braggart who insists that "a nigger's a nigger, on sea or land; and is a coward", that he could have put the nineteen in irons with half a dozen men, and that a whip is better than "all the guns at Old Point". [[Tom Grant]] answers him; Williams is the reason the story of the mutiny is told at all.
