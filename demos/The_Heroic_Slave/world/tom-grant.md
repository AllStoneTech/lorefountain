The first mate of the [[The Creole]]. In the Marine Coffee-house he refuses Jack Williams's theory of negro cowardice and tells what happened: how Madison took the helm, spared his life twice, and sailed them to [[Nassau]]. He concedes that he felt "in the presence of a superior man", and that "our difference of color was the only ground for difference of action." He has resolved never to set foot on a slave ship again.
