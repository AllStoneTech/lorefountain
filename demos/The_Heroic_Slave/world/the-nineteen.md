Madison and the eighteen men whose irons he removed with three files. In the text they number "nineteen in number", hold the deck, command the helm, keep four muskets on the sailors in the rigging, and march off at Nassau "under the triumphant leadership of their heroic chief and deliverer". Only Madison speaks for them aloud; one, "a sturdy black fellow", rushes the mate with a handspike and is checked by Madison.
