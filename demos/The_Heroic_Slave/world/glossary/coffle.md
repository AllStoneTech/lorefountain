---
term: coffle
gloss: A line of enslaved people chained together and driven on foot to market.
aliases: [slave-coffle, gang]
tags: [period-term, slave-trade]
---

The one hundred and thirty at the tavern are a coffle on its way to the Richmond wharf; the text calls it "a slave-gang on their way to market" and later "the slave-coffle".
