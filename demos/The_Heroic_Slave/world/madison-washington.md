The "heroic slave" of Douglass's title: a Virginia-born man who, after a savage whipping, resolves to run, loses the North Star in cloud, lives five years in a swamp, escapes to Canada, and then goes back for his wife and loses her. Sold South in a coffle, he takes the brig [[The Creole]] with eighteen others, spares the first mate's life twice, steers her into [[Nassau]] and walks off her deck at the head of a cheering procession. The narrator admits that only "glimpses of this great character" survive. Everything above comes from the text; his thoughts at sea, between 1x05's hold scene and the revolt, are an adaptation (see `imports/source-material.md`).
