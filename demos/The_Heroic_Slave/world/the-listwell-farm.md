The Ohio home of the Listwells: fireside, centre-table lamp, oak trees, barn, a dog on the watch. Listwell offers "the best room of my house" rather than the barn; Madison sleeps on a real bed for the first time in five years and hesitates whether to lie on the floor instead. Ohio law is "very stringent against any one who should aid... a slave to escape", and the house is where that law is defied.
