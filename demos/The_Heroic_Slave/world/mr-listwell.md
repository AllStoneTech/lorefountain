A Northern traveller who, in spring 1835, hides behind a fallen tree and hears Madison's soliloquy: "From this hour I am an abolitionist." In the winter of 1840 he shelters Madison in his Ohio home and drives him to Cleveland. In 1841, on a second visit to Virginia, he meets him again, in chains, and can only slip him ten dollars and, at the Richmond wharf, three files. Listwell tells the story afterwards; the novella presents him as its source.
