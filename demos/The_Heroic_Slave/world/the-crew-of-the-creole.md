"A dozen hardy seamen" who unfurl the sails at Richmond, and who, when the rising comes, are all aloft in the rigging and will not come down. Madison orders them down with four muskets levelled; when the storm hits, "not a sailor would quit his grasp" to man the pumps. The mate's account of them is unflattering: "frightened monkeys".
