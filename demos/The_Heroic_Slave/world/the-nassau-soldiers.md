"A company of black soldiers" who come aboard the [[The Creole]] at Nassau by order of the authorities, "for the purpose, as they said, of protecting the property". When the mate calls on them to keep the slaves aboard, they answer that their instructions are to protect property and that they "did not recognize persons as property". The mate, narrating, calls them "impudent rascals" and "stupid blockheads". Whether they were from a British West India regiment is a historical inference, not in the text (see `world/notes/the-real-creole.md`).
