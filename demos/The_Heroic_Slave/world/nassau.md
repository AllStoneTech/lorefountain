The British harbour about sixty miles from the point where Madison takes the helm; the brig is "plump at the wharf" two hours after the storm. A crowd of "sympathizing spectators" cheers the nineteen and the others as they march off the ship. In the text, the American consul is sent for but his actions are unknown to the mate; the soldiers do not recognise persons as property. "New Providence" as an alias is historical context only (tentative).
