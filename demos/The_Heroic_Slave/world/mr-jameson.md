"The owner of most of the slaves on board". He is pacing the quarter-deck with [[Captain Clarke]], both armed, when the rising begins. He dies on deck; his body is washed overboard by the first heavy sea of the squall. Madison later says he did no more to the dead men "than they would have done to me in like circumstances."
