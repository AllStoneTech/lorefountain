The Virginia city where, in the text, the great sale of "niggers" is rumoured for the next day, where the coffle is driven to the wharf, and where [[The Creole]] weighs anchor "in the stream" with a hundred and thirty human beings for New Orleans. Two months later its Marine Coffee-house hosts the debate that frames the mutiny story. Historical note: the real Creole sailed from Hampton Roads, not Richmond (see `world/notes/the-real-creole.md`).
