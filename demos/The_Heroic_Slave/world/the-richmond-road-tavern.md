"A somewhat ancient and famous public tavern" on the Petersburg-to-Richmond road, fifteen miles from Richmond, once the resort of gamblers, horse-racers, cock-fighters and slave-traders and now decaying: rags in the upper windows, a rickety portico with "mantraps", loafers like sentinels. [[Mr. Listwell]] sleeps in a room directly over the bar, with the plaster off and nothing but pine boards between him and the talk below; in the morning he finds the gang in the old bowling-alley. The tavern's name is not given.
