The Virginia slaveholder, unnamed, who has Madison lashed, who is away at church on the Sunday morning Listwell overhears Madison, and who, with his two sons, fires on the fleeing pair when Madison returns for Susan. He then has Madison chained to a ring-bolt for three days as a public example, has him given sixty lashes, and sells him to a slave-trader "on condition of my being taken South" — "Their motive is revenge."
