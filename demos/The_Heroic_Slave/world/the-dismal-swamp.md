"In the dismal swamps I lived, sir, five long years, — a cave for my home during the day." Madison wanders at night among wolves and bears and keeps his tinder-box. The wilderness "took fire, and refused longer to be my hiding-place", the conflagration that drives him north. The text says only "dismal swamps", plural and lower-case; identifying it with the real Great Dismal Swamp is a historical inference (see `world/notes/the-real-creole.md`).
