The overseer who has Madison tied to a tree limb with his feet chained and an iron bar between his ankles, administers forty stripes with a cow-skin, and then washes the wounds with old brine "partly to augment my suffering, and partly, as he said, to prevent inflammation". Madison's offence was staying too long at the mill.
