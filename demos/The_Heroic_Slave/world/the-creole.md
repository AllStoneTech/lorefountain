A "long, low, black" Baltimore-built brig that leaves Richmond for New Orleans with a hundred and thirty captives. On the ninth day out, nineteen of them seize her; she ends at the wharf in [[Nassau]]. Aboard: pistols, a binnacle, a caboose, a magazine Madison threatens to light, and below, hatches and irons. As a location it is the drama's last stage and its most sonically rich: wind, timber, rigging, chain.
