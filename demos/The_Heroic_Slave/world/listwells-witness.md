A white man's moral education in three encounters: he is converted by overhearing (1x01), he acts on his conversion with food, a bed and a wagon (1x02), and he fails and recovers at the tavern, where fear makes him hold his tongue, though he still gives his dollar, his ten dollars and his three files (1x04). The text is candid about the middle failure.
