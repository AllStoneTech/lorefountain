"Fourteen men, with something like guns in their hands", who come into the woods on the heels of the praying old man. They accuse him of stealing, tie him to a tree and give him thirty-nine lashes, then scour the woods for an hour, passing within six feet of the hidden Madison, who "held my stick with a firmer grasp".
