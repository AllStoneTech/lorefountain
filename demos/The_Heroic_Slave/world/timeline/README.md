# world/timeline/

In-world events — one file per event, separate from the characters, locations, factions, objects, concepts and arcs that take part in them. Each event can carry two independent positions: where it's presented to the audience (which episode depicts it, via `production_code`) and where it falls in the story's own chronology (`chronological_order`).

This project's timeline leans on that split. The novella tells most of Madison's story as memory: his whipping, the first escape, the five years in the swamp, the swamp fire, the return for Susan and her death are all *told* to Listwell in 1x02–1x04 long after they happened, so their chronological order is early while their episode is late. Likewise the coffee-house frame of 1x05–1x06 comes two months after the voyage it describes.

One event, *Nineteen at work in the hold*, is `canon_status: tentative` because the novella only implies it; the audio drama dramatizes it.
