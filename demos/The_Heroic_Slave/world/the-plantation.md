The unnamed Virginia plantation where Madison is enslaved: the mill, the quarters where contented-seeming fellow slaves sit by a fire ("where there is seeming contentment with slavery, there is certain treachery to freedom"), the great house with Susan's room and its window, and the garden fence where he waits in the dark. Its location within Virginia, its owner's name and its county are all unstated.
