The Canadian town from which Madison writes on December 16, 1840: "I nestle in the mane of the British lion, protected by his mighty paw from the talons and the beak of the American eagle. I AM FREE." It is also where, after his first joy, he cannot work, eat or sleep for thinking of Susan. How long he stayed before returning to Virginia is not stated.
