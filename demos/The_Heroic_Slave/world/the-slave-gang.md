"One hundred and thirty human beings", of every age and sex, chained and fettered at the Richmond-road tavern and driven to the Richmond wharf for shipment to New Orleans. Madison, "by that mesmeric power which is the invariable accompaniment of genius", has already won them over and is "a sort of general-in-chief among them". A girl "not more than twelve years old" weeps and is threatened for it. Historical context for the Creole's real manifest is in `world/notes/the-real-creole.md` (tentative).
