The letter that reaches the Listwells "a few days after" Madison boards the steamer, dated "Windsor, Canada West, Dec. 16, 1840": "Madison is out of the woods at last... I AM FREE, and breathe an atmosphere too pure for slaves, slave-hunters, or slave-holders." It is read aloud in 1x03 and is the last of Madison's own words until the tavern.
