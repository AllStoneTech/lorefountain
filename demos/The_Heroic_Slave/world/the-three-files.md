Three strong files [[Mr. Listwell]] buys at a Richmond hardware store and slips into Madison's pocket at the wharf. Grant later deduces that Madison "must have gone to work upon his fetters the first night out", and that he got the irons off eighteen others besides himself. Their sound (iron on iron, patient and quiet) is the drama's central motif.
