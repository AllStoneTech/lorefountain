# Open questions

Real gaps in the source that the adaptation had to step around rather than answer.

1. **What became of Madison's two children?** In 1x02 he says he left "my poor wife and two little children". They never appear again. The scripts mention them once and do not invent a fate.
2. **Is the praying old man free or enslaved?** Madison meets him among colored woodcutters and he has to account for a dollar. The text never says which.
3. **Who was Madison's master, and where was the plantation?** No names, no county. The scripts keep him anonymous.
4. **What was in the files' sound?** Grant only deduces that Madison filed through his fetters the first night out. The adaptation dramatizes it (tentative) and takes the liberty of giving Madison a moment of silence at his work.
5. **How did Madison learn navigation?** He tells the sailors, "Do not flatter yourselves that I am ignorant of chart or compass." The text never explains. The scripts let Grant wonder aloud and leave it unanswered.
6. **What did the American consul at Nassau do?** The mate says he does not know. We never find out.
7. **Does Listwell ever learn that Madison succeeded?** The novella ends with Grant's account in the coffee-house; Listwell does not appear in Part 4. The scripts do not bring him back.
8. **Whose voice is the narrator?** In the novella it is Douglass in the first person plural. The audio drama gives the role to The Narrator and does not claim to be Douglass.
