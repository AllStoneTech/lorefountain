A Paris jeweller of the Palais Royal (arcade Pierre, No. 45) who buys the Abbé Busoni's diamond from Caderousse on 3 June 1829 for 45,000 francs, returns in the storm, and is stabbed four times in his room at the Pont du Gard inn (ch. 45). He is carrying two small pistols; one is found unfired. Bertuccio, hidden in the shed below, is arrested for the crime. The Count later calls him "Johannes" (ch. 82).
