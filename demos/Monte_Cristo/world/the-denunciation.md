Written by Danglars with his left hand at La Réserve and posted by Fernand (ch. 4). **Continuity:** the text differs a little each time it is quoted (chs. 4, 6, 17). Chapter 5 offers an in-story reason, since Fernand may have copied it. The postmark read by the Englishman in chapter 28 is "27th February".
