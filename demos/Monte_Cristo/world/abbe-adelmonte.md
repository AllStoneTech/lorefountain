A chemist-priest of Taormina in Sicily whom the Count names in conversation with Madame de Villefort: his cabbage, rabbit, hen and vulture experiments, and the elixir that revived Edward, are "a skilful preparation of my friend the worthy Abbé Adelmonte" (ch. 52). He never appears; Madame de Villefort concludes the Count is himself the Adelmonte he talks about, but the book never says so. Marked tentative for that reason.
