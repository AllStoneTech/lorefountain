Inspector of prisons at Marseille for fourteen years, owner of 200,000 francs in Morrel's hands. He sells that debt to the "Englishman" and lets him read the Château d'If register (ch. 28). He is later a high police official in Paris (ch. 69), but in ch. 104 he comes to collect five millions for the charities as "Receiver-General of the charities" — a different office from the police post of ch. 69. In chapter 104 he says Madame de Morcerf and Albert have given all their fortune to the hospitals.
