Private secretary to the Minister of the Interior in 1838, a thin fair man with an eyeglass and the order of Charles III. He is Madame Danglars's regular visitor and Albert's friend at the breakfast. With her money he speculates on information from the ministry (the false telegraph, ch. 61), is accused by Danglars of living on her (ch. 65), and splits their partnership's 2,400,000 francs with her in the little house in the Rue Saint-Germain-des-Prés (ch. 106), keeping 1,060,000; he buys a house on the Boulevard de la Madeleine.
