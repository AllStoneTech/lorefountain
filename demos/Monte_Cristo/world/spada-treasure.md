Two million Roman crowns' worth of gold, bars and jewels in a coffer under a rock on Monte Cristo. Dantès counts a thousand ingots of two to three pounds and 25,000 crowns bearing the effigy of Alexander VI (ch. 24). It funds everything he does after 1829.
