A major in the Austrian service, hired to play the father of the false prince at fifty-two years of age, in the green frock-coat the Count prescribes (ch. 55). He is a gambler at Lucca who is useful only to be a witness. The Count pays him 8,000 francs of a 48,000-franc credit from the "Abbé Busoni" and a 2,400-franc order on Signor Gozzi of Florence (chs. 55–56); he tells Andrea he was paid 50,000 francs down. He is "Marquis Bartolomeo Cavalcanti" to Danglars, who thinks him worth half a million a year (ch. 64); he returns to the baths of Lucca (ch. 76).
