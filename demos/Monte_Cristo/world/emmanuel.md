The one clerk who stayed with Morrel & Son when it collapsed; Sinbad predicts he will marry Julie. By 1838 he is her husband and the Count's host in the Rue Meslay. He and Maximilian are the Count's seconds for the duel (chs. 88, 90). **Continuity:** his surname is printed "Herbaut" (ch. 40) and "Herbault" (ch. 50).
