An "extract of the report of a meeting of the Bonapartist Club in the Rue Saint-Jacques, held February 5th, 1815", signed by Louis-Jacques Beaurepaire, Étienne Duchampy and Claude Lecharpal, kept in a false-bottomed drawer in Noirtier's secretaire. Franz reads it aloud (ch. 75): it proves the General fell in a fair duel and, by the signature of the club's unnamed president, who was Noirtier himself, ends the marriage plan. It also records that an Elba letter promised news "on the arrival of the *Pharaon* belonging to the shipbuilder Morrel".
