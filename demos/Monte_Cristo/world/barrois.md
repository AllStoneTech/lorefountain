Noirtier's faithful servant of twenty-five years, who fetches the notary for the will (chs. 58–59) and is poisoned in chapter 79, aged sixty, by the lemonade meant for his master. He is the only servant who knows the secret spring in Noirtier's secretaire (ch. 75).
