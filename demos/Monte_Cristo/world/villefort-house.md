The procureur's mansion in the Faubourg Saint-Honoré: a courtyard to the street and a chestnut-shaded garden whose boarded iron gate (and the lucerne plot beyond it, which Maximilian rents for 500 francs a year) is where he and Valentine meet (chs. 51, 57, 73). Noirtier lives on the ground floor; Valentine's room opens on Edward's and his mother's, and a library door leads into the house next door, which the Count rents as "Giacomo Busoni" (chs. 94, 100). Four deaths in four months empty it of servants (chs. 72–80, 102); Villefort digs up its garden in madness (ch. 111).
