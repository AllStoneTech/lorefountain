Signor Pastrini's hotel on the Piazza di Spagna, where Albert, Franz and the Count lodge on one landing. **Continuity:** when Danglars reaches Rome in chapter 114 he goes to "the Hôtel d'Espagne" run by "Old Pastrini, our former acquaintance", and the cicerone orders "Casa Pastrini"; chapters 31–37 call Pastrini's house the Hôtel de Londres.
