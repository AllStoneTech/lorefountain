Ali Pasha's favourite soldier, who keeps watch with a lighted match over the powder in the cavern under the Yanina kiosk, ready to blow everything up. He puts out the match on seeing Ali's ring, brought by Fernand's forged pardon, and is stabbed by four soldiers (ch. 77); Haydée names Fernand as his killer (ch. 86).
