The Herbault–Morrel home, "No. 7 in the Rue Meslay" (ch. 50), a white-stone house with a fountain in its court nicknamed "The Little Versailles", bought by Emmanuel; Maximilian has the second floor, Penelon gardens, Cocles is concierge, and the red silk purse sits under a glass cover in the salon. **Continuity:** Maximilian gives the address as "No. 14" at the Morcerf breakfast (ch. 40). The Count reveals himself to the family here (ch. 105).
