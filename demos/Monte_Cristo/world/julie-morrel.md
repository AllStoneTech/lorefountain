Sixteen or seventeen in 1829, she carries Sinbad's letter to the Allées de Meilhan and returns with the red silk purse containing the receipted bill and a diamond marked "Julie's Dowry". By 1838 she is married to Emmanuel. The book's spellings of her married name vary: "Herbaut" at breakfast (ch. 40), "Herbault" in chapter 50.
