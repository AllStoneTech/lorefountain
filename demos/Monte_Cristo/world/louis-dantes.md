Edmond's aged father, a proud old man who lived on sixty francs in three months rather than ask for help. After his son's arrest he refuses to leave his room and dies of hunger; Chapter 27 gives nine days of fasting. In 1838 the Count could not find his grave (ch. 113). **Continuity:** chapter 13 says he died five months after the arrest, while Caderousse in chapter 26 says "about a year" afterwards.
