Noirtier's 900,000 francs in bank scrip, left in a first will to the poor because Valentine will marry Franz d'Épinay (chs. 59–60). After the engagement is broken he has it torn up and makes a second will leaving everything to Valentine on condition she never leaves him; it is then reported that she will have an income of 300,000 livres (ch. 78). D'Avrigny reads the poisoning of Barrois as aimed at Noirtier because the new will was made "the day before yesterday" (ch. 80).
