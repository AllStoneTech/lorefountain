Madeleine Radelle, born near Arles and nicknamed after her village of La Carconte, Caderousse's feverish wife. She urges him to keep quiet in 1829 and, at the end of chapter 27, suspects the diamond is false. She helps Caderousse murder the jeweller [[Joannes]] on 3 June 1829 and is found shot through the throat on the stairs (ch. 45); Caderousse later blames her for the crime (chs. 82–83).
