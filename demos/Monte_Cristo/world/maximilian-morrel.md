Son of the shipowner, a sub-lieutenant nicknamed "the stoic" in 1829 (ch. 30) and by 1838 a captain of Spahis with the Legion of Honor. He saved Château-Renaud at Constantine on 5 September, his father's day of deliverance. He is the Count's closest friend in Paris. He is twenty-nine when he first sees Valentine and thirty-one in 1838 (chs. 73, 79, 105). On the day of Valentine's funeral the Count stops him shooting himself and reveals who he is (ch. 105).
