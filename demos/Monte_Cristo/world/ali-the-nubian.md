The Nubian mute who serves the Count. The Bey of Tunis condemned him to lose his tongue, hand and head; the Count traded a gun and a cutlass for his life (ch. 31). In Paris the Count calls him a slave and a dog he would kill rather than dismiss (ch. 46). He lassoes the runaway grays and saves Madame de Villefort and Edward (ch. 47), loads the Count's pistols, helps him in the burglary night (ch. 82), drives the britzka to Normandy (ch. 85), carries the Count's letter to Noirtier (ch. 112) and leaves with Haydée (ch. 111). His name also occurs inside "Ali Baba" and "Ali Pasha", which link to this card by plain-text match unless the longer name is defined.
