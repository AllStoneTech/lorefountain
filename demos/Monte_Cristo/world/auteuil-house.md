The house at 28 Rue de la Fontaine, bought by the Count for fifty thousand francs in chapter 42. It is where Villefort buried his newborn son, Bertuccio stabbed Villefort and found the child, and the Count's dinner takes place (chs. 62–63; the skeleton of an infant the Count announces there is his invention, ch. 67). Caderousse is not killed here: he dies at the Count's town house, [[the Champs-Élysées house]]. The Count tells Paris the burglary happened while he was at Auteuil (ch. 84). Chapters 63 and 67 give how long the house stood empty as ten years and nine, against five since the Marquis last visited (ch. 43).
