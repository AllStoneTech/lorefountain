Villefort's daughter, engaged to Franz d'Épinay by her father but in love with Maximilian Morrel. Poisoned repeatedly (she has been drinking Noirtier's brucine mixture to build up resistance, ch. 93), she is watched over by the Count through the library door, drinks his narcotic and apparently dies in chapter 102, and is taken to Monte Cristo island, where Maximilian finds her in the last chapter. **Continuity:** her age is "scarcely eighteen" (ch. 48), "nineteen" (ch. 52) and "about to enter her nineteenth year" (ch. 58); her mother Renée is said to have died twenty-one years before 1838 (ch. 43), which cannot be reconciled with a daughter born around 1819–20. See [[Renée de Saint-Méran]].
