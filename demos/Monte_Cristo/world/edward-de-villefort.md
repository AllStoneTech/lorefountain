The spoiled young son of Villefort and Héloïse, whose life the Count saves in chapter 47. About seven or eight (ch. 47). His mother poisons him and herself, leaving a note: "A good mother cannot depart without her son" (ch. 111); the Count says that his death changed him, and the letter in ch. 117 says Edward "died last September with his mother".
