The port where the book opens on the arrival of the *Pharaon* and where Dantès is arrested; the setting of chapters 1–13 and 25–30. It holds the Palais de Justice (adjoining Villefort's house), the Allées de Meilhan, the Rue de Noailles and the quay. Dantès' denunciation is posted here.
