Ali Pasha's favourite wife, a Christian, whose name Haydée says means "royal". She is with Ali at the kiosk on the Lake of Yanina, is sold with her daughter to the slave-merchant El-Kobbir by the French officer Fernand, and dies at the gates of Constantinople on seeing her husband's head displayed there (ch. 77).
