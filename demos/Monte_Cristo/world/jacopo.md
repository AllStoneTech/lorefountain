A Corsican sailor who pulls Dantès out of the water by the hair and who refuses his prize money. Dantès teaches him navigation and gives him a boat; he sails it to Marseille to ask after old Dantès and Mercédès, and later crews the Count's yacht ("Jacopo, Jacopo, Jacopo!", ch. 30).
