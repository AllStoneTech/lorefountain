The Count's French valet de chambre, in his service a year when the Count lectures him on discretion (ch. 46) and paid 1,500 francs a year; he announces visitors and travels with him when he leaves Paris (ch. 111). He is one of three servants who answer the gong (Ali, Baptistin, Bertuccio).
