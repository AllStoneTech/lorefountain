Dantès's tailor neighbor, who watched the denunciation written and said nothing. By 1829 he keeps the Pont du Gard inn and sells the diamond to the jeweller [[Joannes]] for 45,000 francs (ch. 44); he and La Carconte murder the jeweller that night (ch. 45), and he is later condemned to the galleys for life. He escapes from Saint-Mandrier near Toulon with Benedetto, freed by "Lord Wilmore". In 1838, living as a "retired baker" named Pailletin in the Rue Ménilmontant, he blackmails the false Cavalcanti (chs. 64, 81), tries to rob the Count's house at 30 Avenue des Champs-Élysées (not Auteuil) and is stabbed outside its garden wall by Benedetto; the Count, as the Abbé Busoni, watches him die ("One!", chs. 82–83). **Continuity:** ch. 2 gives his age as 25 or 26 and Fernand's as 21, yet ch. 13 calls him "eight years older" (ch. 81 has him fifty in 1838). His convict number is "No. 106" in ch. 64 but "No. 58" in ch. 82. See [[Caderousse's age]].
