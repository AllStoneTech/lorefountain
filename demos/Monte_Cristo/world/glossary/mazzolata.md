---
term: mazzolata
gloss: The Roman execution in which the condemned is struck down with a mace before his throat is cut.
aliases: [mazzolato, mandaïa]
tags: [roman, execution]
---

The fate of Andrea Rondolo on the Piazza del Popolo (ch. 35); the *mandaïa* is the Roman guillotine used for Peppino's sentence.
