---
term: brucine
gloss: A vegetable poison taken from the false angostura, which d'Avrigny gives Noirtier in doses and which the Count says one can take in rising amounts to become immune.
aliases: [strychnine]
tags: [poison]
---

The Count teaches the method to Madame de Villefort (ch. 52); d'Avrigny prescribes it to Noirtier for his paralysis (ch. 73), and Noirtier gives it to Valentine in small doses, which is why she survives the first attempt (chs. 93–94). The book names brucine or strychnine for the Saint-Méran deaths (ch. 73).
