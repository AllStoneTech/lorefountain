---
term: aqua Tofana
gloss: A famous poison whose secret, Madame de Villefort says, certain people in Perugia were thought to have preserved.
aliases: [aqua tofana]
tags: [poison]
---

Madame de Villefort questions the Count about it in the garden of the Hôtel des Postes at Perugia on Corpus Christi day (ch. 52); in the Locusta chapter he tells Valentine that "ever since then the infernal project has been ripening" in her stepmother's brain (ch. 101).
