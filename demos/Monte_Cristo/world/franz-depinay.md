A baron who has lived three or four years in Italy and is Albert's guide at the Roman Carnival. He dines with Sinbad on Monte Cristo (ch. 31), overhears the Count at the Colosseum, and keeps his secret. Engaged to Valentine, he breaks off the match by letter after Noirtier tells him he killed Franz's father (chs. 75, 78). His father [[General de Quesnel]] died in a duel on 5 February 1815, when Franz was two, so Franz is about twenty-five in 1838 (chs. 58, 75). Albert asks him to be a second and he attends the aborted duel (chs. 78, 90).
