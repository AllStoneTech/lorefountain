The editor whose paper is "the terror of the French government". He prints the Yanina story in *l'Impartial* (ch. 78), asks three weeks' delay (to 21 September), travels to Yanina for the proofs, and burns them with Albert to spare him (ch. 84); another paper then prints the story (ch. 85). He serves as Albert's second (ch. 90).
