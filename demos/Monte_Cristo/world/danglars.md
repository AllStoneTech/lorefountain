Supercargo of the *Pharaon* and author, with the left hand, of the denunciation. Caderousse later reports him a Spanish banker's cashier, a baron and a millionaire. In 1838 the Count opens an unlimited credit with him and drains it; Danglars loses his fortune and his daughter, and is kidnapped by Luigi Vampa's men and made to pay for each meal. His hair turns white when he is freed (ch. 116). In 1838 he is a baron, chevalier of the Legion of Honor and an opposition deputy, "past fifty" (ch. 46); he loses 500,000 francs on the false telegraph (ch. 61; he says 700,000 in chs. 65–66) and 600,000 + 400,000 on Jacopo Manfredi of Trieste (ch. 66), refuses Albert for Eugénie after writing to Yanina on the Count's hint (chs. 66, 78), draws five million-franc bonds into the Count's hands on the morning of Valentine's funeral and flees with 50,000 francs and a passport (ch. 104). He leaves Rome with 5,050,000 francs and pays 100,000 francs a fowl until 50,000 is left (chs. 114–116). The Count lets him keep the 50,000 and says the hospitals' five millions were restored by "an unknown hand".
