The island fortress off Marseille where Dantès spends fourteen years (chs. 8–21). "The sea is the cemetery of the Château d'If": the dead are weighted and thrown from the rocks, which is how he escapes. In 1838 it holds no prisoners (none since the July Revolution); the Count visits his cell (No. 34) and Faria's (No. 27), hears the jailers' version of the escape and takes away Faria's manuscript on strips of cloth (ch. 113).
