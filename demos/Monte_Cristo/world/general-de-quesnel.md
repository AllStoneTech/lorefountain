Franz's father, who served the Emperor from 1804 to 1814 and was made a baron and marshal by Louis XVIII, with the estate of Épinay. Lured blindfold to a Bonapartist club in the Rue Saint-Jacques on 5 February 1815, he refuses to join, is forced into a sword duel on the Quai des Ormes with the club's president and dies of his wounds; his body is thrown in the river. Villefort and everyone else call it an assassination until Noirtier's statement is read (ch. 75).
