The seaside house with a park and a creek that Bertuccio buys in Normandy for the Count (chs. 46, 82), where the sloop with the Monte Cristo arms lies. The Count and Albert drive there with relays of Hungarian-bred horses in eight hours and hunt and fish for three days (ch. 85). It is among the gifts in the Count's last letter (ch. 117).
