Daughter of the Marquis and Marquise de Saint-Méran and Villefort's fiancée, who asks him to be merciful on the day of their betrothal. His sentimental promise sits uneasily with what he does next. Villefort calls her his first wife and the mother of [[Valentine de Villefort]] (ch. 52). She follows his litter south in 1817 (ch. 67), is buried at Père-Lachaise, and gives the Saint-Méran and Villefort families one tomb (ch. 105). **Continuity:** the Auteuil concierge says she died "one-and-twenty years ago" (ch. 43), but ch. 74 says her parents rejoin her "after ten years of separation"; and Valentine, her daughter, is eighteen or nineteen in 1838.
