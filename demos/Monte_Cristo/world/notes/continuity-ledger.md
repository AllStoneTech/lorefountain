# Continuity ledger: where the book disagrees with itself

Each item was checked against the Gutenberg text in `imports/the-count-of-monte-cristo.md`. Pairs are quoted by chapter number; the matching entity or timeline card is marked `canon_status: contradicted`. Not every slip is necessarily Dumas's: some come from the serial publication and some from translation. Items 1–12 come from the first reading (chapters 1–42), items 13–31 from the full reading of chapters 43–117.

1. **Day of the arrest.** Chapter 8 says "the 1st of March". Chapters 14, 15 and 21 say 28 February 1815, "fourteen years, day for day". Chapter 1 gives the *Pharaon*'s arrival as 24 February, but chapter 5 says "arrived only yesterday morning" and the postmark in chapter 28 is 27 February.
2. **When old Dantès died.** Five months after the arrest (ch. 13) versus "about a year" (ch. 26).
3. **Caderousse's age.** 25 or 26 against Fernand's 21 (ch. 2), yet "eight years older" (ch. 13). (Chapter 81 has him fifty in 1838, which fits 25–26 in 1815.)
4. **Dantès's years before the tunnel.** "Nearly four years" and "the six years" in the same chapter (ch. 15).
5. **Faria's arrest.** 1807 (ch. 18) versus 1811 (chs. 14, 16); Spada's secretary for twenty years (ch. 14) or ten (ch. 18).
6. **Weekdays in Rome.** Franz arrives "Saturday evening" (ch. 32); next morning it is "Thursday" (ch. 33); the printed notice says "Wednesday, February 23rd" (ch. 34).
7. **House numbers on the Rue Meslay.** Morrel gives "No. 14" at the breakfast (ch. 40); the house the Count visits is "No. 7" (ch. 50).
8. **Emmanuel's surname.** "Herbaut" (ch. 40) and "Herbault" (ch. 50).
9. **Boville's debt.** Two hundred thousand francs, half on the 15th of each month (ch. 28); the sums then quoted as due on the 15th are 100,000 in chapter 29 and "50,000" in chapter 30.
10. **The chamberlain's name.** Printed "Salvieux" (ch. 6) and "Servieux" (ch. 27, 54) for the chamberlain; the baroness signs "née Hermine de Servieux". Chapter 46 spells the baroness's maiden name "de Servières" (see 15).
11. **Faria's death date.** The inspector says Faria died "five or six months ago, last February" (ch. 28), but the date is 5 June, about three and a half months later.
12. **The denunciation's wording.** Different each time it is quoted (chs. 4, 6, 17, 89). Chapter 5 gives an explanation, since Fernand may have recopied it.
13. **When Renée died.** "One-and-twenty years ago" (ch. 43, the concierge); alive and following Villefort's litter in the autumn of 1817 (ch. 67); her parents join her "after ten years of separation" (ch. 74). Her daughter Valentine is "scarcely eighteen" (ch. 48), "nineteen" (ch. 52), "about to enter her nineteenth year" (ch. 58), which fits only the "ten years".
14. **Madame Danglars's first husband.** "Colonel the Marquis of Nargonne" (ch. 46) against a baron whose coronet is on the foundling's napkin (chs. 44, 67).
15. **Her maiden name.** "De Servières" (ch. 46) against "de Servieux" (chs. 27, 54).
16. **The night Benedetto was born.** "The 20th of September" (ch. 67, Villefort) against "the 27th of September, 1817" (ch. 110, Benedetto); Bertuccio says "the end of September" (ch. 44).
17. **When Assunta took him back.** About six months later (ch. 67) or three months (ch. 110); when Bertuccio comes home the baby is "seven or eight months old" (ch. 44).
18. **Caderousse's convict number.** "No. 106" (ch. 64), "No. 58" (ch. 82).
19. **Andrea's mother.** "Oliva Corsinari" (chs. 55–56); "Leonora Corsinari" (ch. 81).
20. **How long Noirtier has taken brucine.** Three months (ch. 73), twelve (ch. 80), three years (ch. 100).
21. **Haydée's sale.** Sold at thirteen to the Sultan (ch. 77); El-Kobbir's receipt in the Chamber of Peers (ch. 86) says she was eleven and had been sold to him seven years earlier.
22. **Pastrini's hotel.** The Hôtel de Londres (chs. 31–37) but the Hôtel d'Espagne when Danglars arrives (ch. 114).
23. **The Count's horses.** The Count says Maximilian's Médéah cost 5,000 francs (ch. 62); Maximilian paid 4,500 (ch. 57).
24. **Danglars's telegraph loss.** 500,000 francs (narrator, ch. 61), 700,000 (Danglars, chs. 65, 66).
25. **The Auteuil house.** It has been empty "ten years" (Château-Renaud, ch. 63), "twenty years" (the Count, ch. 60), nine years in a lease (Villefort, ch. 67), while the concierge says the Marquis was last there five years ago (ch. 43).
26. **Boville's office.** A high police official (ch. 69); Receiver-General of the charities (ch. 104).
27. **Where Mercédès and Albert lodge.** Albert installs his mother in "a small furnished house in the Rue des Saints-Pères" (ch. 91) which chapter 106 places in the Rue Saint-Germain-des-Prés.
28. **Years since the Morrel rescue.** The rescue was on 5 September 1829 (ch. 30). In 1838 the Count says "ten years today" (ch. 105), "eleven years" (chs. 105, 112) and, in ch. 113, that he looked for his father's grave "ten years ago".
29. **Danglars's money at Rome.** The Count's receipt is for 5,100,000 francs (ch. 104); Danglars's letter of credit is for 5,050,000 (chs. 114–115) although he left Paris with 50,000 in notes.
30. **The calendar of 1838.** "Today is the 30th" is the day after the Saturday Auteuil dinner (ch. 66), though the Cavalcantis arrive on Tuesday 26 May (chs. 54–56). The ball is in July (chs. 70–71) and the events that follow it are a few days apart, yet Beauchamp says "29th of August" in ch. 78, and the Count says it is "the 5th of September" on the day of Valentine's funeral (ch. 105), which must come after Beauchamp's deadline of 21 September.
31. **The ages of Mercédès's son.** "Not yet twenty-one" (ch. 54), twenty-one (ch. 78), "scarcely twenty-two" (ch. 91).

Not claimed: any slip that the text explains. Not a slip: the burglary and Caderousse's death are at the Count's Champs-Élysées house (chs. 82–83), not at Auteuil; Vampa reads Cæsar in the catacombs (ch. 37) and Plutarch's *Alexander* (ch. 114), both reading, no contradiction.
