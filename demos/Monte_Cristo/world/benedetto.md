Born, he says, on the night of 27 September 1817 at 28 Rue de la Fontaine, Auteuil; his father buried him alive and a vendetta-seeker dug him up (ch. 110). A convict who escaped and killed Caderousse, he is introduced by the Count as Andrea Cavalcanti and nearly marries Eugénie Danglars. Arrested at the Bell and Bottle in Compiègne (not in Belgium, ch. 98), he denounces Villefort as his father at his trial (ch. 110). Before that: raised at Rogliano by [[Assunta]] and Bertuccio's household (chs. 44–45), a forger at sixteen and a galley convict at Toulon (No. 59, chs. 82–83), freed with Caderousse by "Lord Wilmore". **Continuity:** the night of his birth is "the 20th of September" in Villefort's account (ch. 67) but "the 27th of September, 1817" in his own (ch. 110); Bertuccio says only "the end of September" (ch. 44). Assunta reclaims him after about six months (ch. 67) or three (ch. 110).
