Owner of the *Pharaon* and head of Morrel & Son, thirty-six when the book opens and fifty by 1829. He pleads for Dantès, pays for old Dantès's burial, and on 5 September 1829 is about to shoot himself when Julie brings the purse that saves him. Surnames: [[Maximilian Morrel]] also answers to "Morrel", so bare mentions link to both cards. His uncle Policar Morrel served with Napoleon (ch. 1). His last words are "Maximilian, it was Edmond Dantès!" (ch. 50); he died in his children's arms and lies in Marseille beside his wife, who died two years earlier (ch. 113).
