Chapter 18 has Faria find the will on Christmas Day 1807 and be arrested "a month" later. Chapters 14 and 16 say he has been in the Château d'If since 1811 after three years in Fenestrelle. Both can be true only if the 1811 date is the move to the Château d'If.
