Barrois runs to fetch Maximilian, drinks the glass of lemonade Valentine offers him and dies; d'Avrigny shows with syrup of violets that the decanter is poisoned and tells Villefort the poison was meant for Noirtier, whom years of brucine have protected (chs. 79–80).
