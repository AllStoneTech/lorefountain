Veiled, Mercédès comes to the Count's study, calls him Edmond, and begs for her son's life. He shows her Danglars's denunciation, bought for 200,000 francs from the prison file, promises Albert shall live, and decides to let himself be shot. She had recognised him from the first, "by the simple sound of your voice".
