Breaking Maximilian's window to stop his suicide, the Count says he is the man who saved Pierre Morrel and sent the purse, "the Edmond Dantès who nursed you, a child, on my knees". Maximilian calls Julie and Emmanuel; Julie asks to keep the purse. The Count extracts a month's promise. **Conflict:** the Count says it is "the 5th of September, ten years today since I saved your father" and that they have been friends "eleven years", though the rescue was on 5 September 1829 (nine years earlier, ch. 30); and 5 September cannot be reconciled with chapter 78's "29th of August" and the weeks that follow it (see [[the calendar of September 1838]]).
