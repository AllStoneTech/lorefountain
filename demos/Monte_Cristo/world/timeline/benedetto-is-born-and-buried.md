Told in Bertuccio's story (chs. 43–45), Madame Danglars's interview (ch. 67) and Benedetto's own account at his trial (ch. 110): Villefort buries the newborn at 28 Rue de la Fontaine and Bertuccio digs him up. Villefort tells Madame Danglars the foundling hospital received a child "the night of the 20th of September" (ch. 67); Benedetto says he was born "the night of the 27th of September, 1817" (ch. 110); Bertuccio says "the end of September".
