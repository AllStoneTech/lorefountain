The Auteuil concierge says Villefort's first wife died "one-and-twenty years ago" (ch. 43); in 1817 she follows Villefort's litter south (ch. 67); and when her parents are buried beside her "after ten years of separation" (ch. 74). Valentine, Renée's daughter, is eighteen or nineteen in 1838 (chs. 48, 52, 58).
