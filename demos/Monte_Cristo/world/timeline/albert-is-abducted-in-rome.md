Albert follows a "peasant girl" (really Beppo) and is held in the catacombs for four thousand piastres; the Count frees him. The weekdays in this sequence disagree: Franz arrives "Saturday evening" (ch. 32) and is told next morning "today is Thursday" (ch. 33), and the notice of chapter 34 reads "Wednesday, February 23rd".
