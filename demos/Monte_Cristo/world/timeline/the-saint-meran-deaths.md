The Marquis dies in his carriage on the road to Paris (reported chs. 71–72); the Marquise reaches Paris with his body, asks for a notary, drinks her usual orangeade and dies in three attacks as the contract is to be signed (chs. 72–73). D'Avrigny tells Villefort in the garden it is poison, Maximilian overhears from the trees, and Villefort refuses an inquest. Both are buried at Père-Lachaise on one day (ch. 74).
