The Count bribes the telegraph operator at Montlhéry with 25,000 francs to send three changed signals (ch. 61). Debray hears of "Don Carlos's escape" through the Minister of the Interior and warns Madame Danglars, who has her husband sell his Spanish bonds; next day the *Moniteur* denies it. **Conflict:** the narrator gives Danglars's loss as 500,000 francs (ch. 61); Danglars himself says 700,000 (chs. 65, 66).
