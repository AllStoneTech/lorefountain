Caderousse breaks into the Count's town house at 30 Avenue des Champs-Élysées after a warning letter has brought the Count back there, disguised as the Abbé Busoni (ch. 82). Released through the window, he is stabbed three times outside the garden wall by Benedetto, who has been watching. The Count carries him in, writes his deposition naming the Corsican Benedetto (galley convict No. 59), reveals himself to him by whispering a name, and says "One!" as he dies (ch. 83). The murder is not at Auteuil.
