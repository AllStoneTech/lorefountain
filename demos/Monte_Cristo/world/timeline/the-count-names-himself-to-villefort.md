In Noirtier's room the Abbé Busoni removes his wig and tells Villefort "You heard my voice first at Marseilles twenty-three years ago", and gives the name Edmond Dantès. Villefort drags him upstairs to the corpses of his wife and son ("are you well avenged?"); the Count carries Edward into Valentine's room, fails to revive him, and leaves Villefort digging in the garden (ch. 111).
