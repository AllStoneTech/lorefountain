Chapters 70–71 put the Morcerf ball in the first days of July, and chapters 72–78 follow on from it in a few days (the Saint-Méran funerals, the contract, Franz's letter), yet in chapter 78 Beauchamp says "Today is the 29th of August" and sets 21 September as the end of his three weeks. Chapter 105 then has the Count say it is "the 5th of September", the day of Valentine's funeral, a month before the appointment of 5 October (chs. 113, 117); but Valentine's death comes after the 21 September deadline (chs. 84–93). Both the "July" and the "5th of September" sit badly with the chain of events.
