Dantès is arrested at the betrothal feast. **Conflicts:** chapter 1 dates the *Pharaon*'s arrival 24 February, but chapter 5 says "arrived only yesterday morning" and the postmark in chapter 28 reads 27 February. Chapter 8 says "the 1st of March", while chapters 14, 15 and 21 give "the 28th of February", fourteen years to the day before the escape.
