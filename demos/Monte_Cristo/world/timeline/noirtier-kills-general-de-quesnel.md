Lured blindfold to the Bonapartist club in the Rue Saint-Jacques and refusing to swear, General de Quesnel fights a sword duel with the club's president on the Quai des Ormes and dies; his body is thrown in the river. Everyone, including Villefort, calls it an assassination until Franz reads the signed statement in chapter 75 and Noirtier names himself as the president. It happens three weeks before the *Pharaon* reaches Marseille.
