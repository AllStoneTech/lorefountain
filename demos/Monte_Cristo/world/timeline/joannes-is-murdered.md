Told by Bertuccio in chapter 44–45: the jeweller pays 45,000 francs for the Abbé Busoni's diamond, is invited to stay when the storm drives him back, and is killed in his bed by Caderousse and La Carconte. Caderousse flees with the money and the diamond; Bertuccio, who was hidden in the shed, is arrested and freed on 8 September when Busoni confirms his story. Caderousse is caught and sent to the galleys for life.
