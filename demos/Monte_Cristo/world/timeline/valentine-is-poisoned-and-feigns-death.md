The Count has watched four nights from the next house. Madame de Villefort pours poison into Valentine's glass, the Count gives Valentine a narcotic pastille, and at dawn d'Avrigny pronounces her dead (chs. 100–102). Maximilian denounces the murder to Villefort, d'Avrigny and Noirtier (ch. 103); the Abbé Busoni watches the body and Valentine is buried at Père-Lachaise (chs. 104–105). The Count restores her on 5 October (ch. 117).
