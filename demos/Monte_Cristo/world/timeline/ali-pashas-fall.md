Told by Haydée to Albert (ch. 77) and again to the Chamber of Peers (ch. 86): Ali Pasha withdraws to the kiosk on the lake with powder and gold, sends the French officer Fernand to Constantinople, and Fernand returns with a forged pardon and Ali's ring. Selim obeys the ring and is killed, Ali is shot to pieces, and Fernand sells Vasiliki and Haydée to El-Kobbir for 400,000 francs; Ali's head is shown at the gate of Constantinople.
