Wife of the banker, formerly the widow of a Nargonne (Caderousse names her "Madame de Nargonne, daughter of M. de Servieux", and she signs "Hermine de Servieux"). Chapter 67 reveals to her that the child she believed dead was buried alive at Auteuil; Benedetto's trial exposes her (she faints in court, ch. 110). Her husband says she and Debray have drawn 500,000 livres from his speculations this year (ch. 65); after Danglars flees, Debray hands her 1,340,000 francs, her half of their partnership (ch. 106). **Continuity:** her maiden name is "de Servières" in ch. 46, "de Servieux" in chs. 27 and 54; her first husband is "Colonel the Marquis of Nargonne" in ch. 46 but a baron (the napkin's coronet, "Monsieur de Nargonne was a baron") in ch. 67.
