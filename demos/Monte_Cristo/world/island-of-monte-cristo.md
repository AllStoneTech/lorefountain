A deserted rock between Corsica and Elba, belonging to Tuscany, where Cardinal Spada hid his treasure. Dantès finds it in chapter 24 and later buys the island and takes its name for his title. Franz dines in its hidden grotto in chapter 31, and the last chapter ends here. The Count's will (written at dawn before the duel) puts twenty millions in the grotto at Maximilian's disposal, with Bertuccio knowing the secret (ch. 90); there on 5 October he gives Maximilian the "poison" that is a narcotic and restores Valentine (ch. 117). The Abbé Busoni calls Monte Cristo "a rock", not a family name (ch. 69).
