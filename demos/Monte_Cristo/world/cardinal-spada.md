The Cardinal Cæsar Spada of 1498, invited to dine with Pope Alexander VI and Cæsar Borgia and poisoned. His nephew Guido Spada also dies. He buried his wealth on Monte Cristo and left a will on a paper used as a marker in the family breviary. (Faria also speaks of a later Count of Spada, his patron.)
