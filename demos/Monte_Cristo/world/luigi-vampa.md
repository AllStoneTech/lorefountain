A former shepherd of twenty-two who killed the bandit Cucumetto and took his place at the head of a band near Rome. Pastrini tells his story in chapter 33. He abducts Albert and then frees him for the Count, reads Cæsar's *Commentaries* in the catacombs, and in 1838 later holds Danglars to ransom at his hideout, where he is found reading Plutarch's *Life of Alexander* (chs. 114–116). The "Vampa" who wins the Jockey Club cup in Paris (ch. 53) is the Count's horse, named after him.
