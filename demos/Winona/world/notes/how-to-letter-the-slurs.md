# How to letter the slurs

The novel's white characters say "nigger" often and its Black characters say it rarely. This comic keeps every use that the novel gives a speaker, in that speaker's mouth only, and never in a caption box (the narrator in the novel does not use it).

**Open questions for the letterer and editor.**

- Typeset it plain, or visually marked (smaller, in the speaker's own balloon style)? Proposal: plain, same size, in the speaker's balloon. A marker would be an editorial comment the source does not make.
- Dialect: Maybee, Tennie, 'Tavius and Aunt Vinnie speak in heavy eye-dialect. Keep it exactly as written, lettered clearly; do not smooth Maybee's and do not exaggerate the others'.
- Maybee uses the word while risking his life for the people he says it about. The scripts keep both. If a reader sees only one panel they will see only the word.
- "Squaw" and "half-breed" (Nokomis, Brown's "pretty squaw") and "mulattress" and "quadroon" (Winona, her mother, Allen Pinks) are kept as the novel has them.
