# Dating the Erie chapters

Chapter I says "the early 50's". Chapter III has Maybee tell Warren the slave traders acted "under the new act for the rendition of fugitive slaves jes' passed by Congress"; the Fugitive Slave Act is 1850. Chapter IV then opens "early in the spring of 1856" and says Winona and Judah have been captives for "two years"; Chapter VI has Warren say "second trip to America in two years" and Thomson say "our hunting trip up at Erie two years ago". That puts the murder in 1854, four years after the act, not "just passed".

Chapter XII also has the judge set a hanging for "Friday, the 26th day of May next", which is a Friday in 1854 (and 1865) but a Monday in 1856. The novel gives only the one year (1856) and the Ranger-and-Brown events that fit it.

**Open question.** The comic prints only the novel's own "early 50's" on page 1 of Issue 1 and "early in the spring of 1856" on the Magnolia Farm splash of Issue 2 (page 12); it prints no year for the murder or for the traders' act. If the owner wants a caption between them it should say "two years before" rather than guess. Do not "fix" the traders' act into 1854 in any caption.
