# Page budget

An American monthly is 22 story pages. The novel is about 44,000 words in seventeen chapters, short for a series of 22-page issues. The scripts here are eight issues of 18 to 23 pages, 160 pages in all, about two chapters an issue. The beats are all there; what is missing is the novel's narrator and its long descriptions of woods and weather.

The ratio of pages to chapters is uneven on purpose: Issue 1 is two chapters because the novel's opening is quiet and a first issue needs room; Issue 2 is three chapters (III, IV and V) because the island's aftermath and the plantation have to be told in one go; Issue 6 stops at the embrace on the peak and Issue 7 opens on the messenger, so the battle and the ledge sit in one issue.

**Open questions.**

- Keep eight issues, or fold Issue 7 into 6 and 8 and publish a six-issue run with two 28-page specials?
- Issue 5 (the stake, the trial and the nursing, Chapters X to XII) is the densest and is 22 pages; a 24-page version would give the nurse scenes room.
- Splash pages are placed at 1x01 (page 1, the lake; page 18, the boat in the storm), 1x02 (page 12, Magnolia Farm; page 19, Judah and the horse), 1x03 (page 17, the drop from the *Crescent*), 1x05 (page 5, the stake), 1x07 (page 8, the ledge), 1x08 (page 19, the jubilee).
