# The Brown question

The novel makes John Brown a loving figure, "pastor, guide and counsellor", and then has his men shoot the captured Rangers at sunrise with the narrator's blessing ("the rough justice of the times"). Warren pleads for most of them and wins; the rest die in a volley during Thomson's last prayer.

**Open question.** The comic stays with the novel: the volley is a sound lettered twice (Issue 7 at sunrise, Issue 8 over the prayer), with a line from Steward at Maybee's bedside ("Captain Brown is a shootin' the pris'ners") and his gruff "You ain't goin' nowhere, partner." No panel shows the shooting. Keep that restraint, or draw it?
