Proprietor of the Grand Island Hotel, justice of the peace and town constable near Buffalo, and a conductor on the Underground Railroad who talks like a man who despises the people he is helping. He leads the first night's rescue across the storm-lit lake, turns up two years later on the Kansas City wharf as a John Brown man, plans the escape from the *Crescent*, and is carried home wounded in an improvised ambulance. He keeps White Eagle's papers safe with his wife.
