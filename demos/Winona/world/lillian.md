The Colonel's invalid daughter, "a crippled girl in her rolling chair" who watches the cotton hands from the back porch. Because of her feelings, no slave is whipped at Magnolia Farm; they are sent to the slave prison in Kansas City instead. She is the reason, Thomson says at his death, that her father did the murder on the island.
