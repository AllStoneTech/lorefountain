Judah's oath on the night of the murder ("it'll be him or me"), narrowed by two years of the whip into an oath against Thomson. It is stayed in the jail by John Brown's "vengeance is mine", answered in the Brown camp by the promise "that he shall be given to me as my right", carried out on the ledge on a count of three, and finally taken out of his hands by Winona by the river ("You make yourself as vile as the vilest of them").
