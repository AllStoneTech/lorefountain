The "fighting parson" of Kansas, Sampson Steward: station-keeper on the Underground Railroad, leader of an armed congregation, and singer of "A charge to keep I have". He hides Winona in his loft and Judah under his bed, gives Warren the argument for self-defense, and is shot in the ambush. Everyone thinks him dead for eight weeks. He rides back to the battle at Reynolds's side.
