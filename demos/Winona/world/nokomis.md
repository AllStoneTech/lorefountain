White Eagle's old half-deaf housekeeper on the island. She tells Winona that the Indian-pipes are spirits that turn black when touched, and she is the one who says, over the body, "White Eagle has answered the call of the Great Spirit." Winona later quotes her on men ("they are all the same"). The novel does not say what became of her when the children were taken.
