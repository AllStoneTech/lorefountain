John Brown's encampment in a cup-shaped basin of wooded hills near Lawrence, on a creek, entered by rock-edged ravines "where a few men could hold the entrance against great odds". Rude shelter-huts of branches stand in the shadows; the women cook at a fire; pickets (two of Brown's sons) fire warnings from rock to rock. On the hillside above is a cave, found by Winona, that holds supplies and the women and children when the Rangers come. The Possawatomie runs below.
