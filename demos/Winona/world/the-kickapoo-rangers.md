The border-ruffian company Thomson drills at the Colonel's farm "to burn and loot the infant territory and carry it into the slave-holding lines by fire and fraud". Maybee blames them for the tongue-pulling of Bud Wilson's wife, for the death of Captain R. P. Brown at Easton and for the hanging of three men at Oscaloosa. They are routed at the old farmhouse and the survivors shot.
