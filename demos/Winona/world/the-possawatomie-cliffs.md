The highest peak above Brown's camp, with a sheer drop of "many feet" to the Possawatomie on one side, where Warren keeps lookout and nearly loses Winona; and, across the same mountain, the narrow ledge sixty feet above the river from which Thomson is made to jump. In summer the river is only "a silver thread".
