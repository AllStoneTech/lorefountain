The informal chain of hiding places that moves fugitives from Missouri to Canada: Steward's cabin with its loft trapdoor behind dried apples, the Brown camp, Maybee's hotel. Steward explains to Warren: "A fugitive is passed along by us, night after night, until he secures his freedom. Our methods are a profound mystery." The novel first names Buffalo "the last and most convenient station".
