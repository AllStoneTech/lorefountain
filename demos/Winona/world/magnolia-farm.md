Colonel Titus's plantation a few miles out from Kansas City, Missouri: a rambling two-storey frame house wrapped in piazzas, roses, honeysuckle, grape-vines and magnolia, a high strong fence around four acres, a thousand acres of cotton running to the muddy Missouri, and the slave quarters under the pines behind. In the spring of 1856 the household is the Colonel, his daughter Lillian, Thomson and Mrs. Thomson. Judah and Winona live there for two years.
