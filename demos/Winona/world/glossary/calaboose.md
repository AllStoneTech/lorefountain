---
term: Calaboose
gloss: A local lock-up; the novel's word for the jail where Allen Pinks says he was first held on suspicion of being a fugitive.
aliases: [bell gate, stove-hole]
tags: [period-term, prison]
---

Also in the glossary for the *bell gate* of the Kansas City slave prison where Judah delivers the note, and the *stove-hole* in the floor of Warren's cell through which he sees and speaks to the slaves below.
