---
term: Quadroon
gloss: The period term Maybee uses for Winona, a person of one-quarter Black ancestry; the novel also calls her mother a "mulattress".
aliases: [mulattress, mulatto]
tags: [period-term, race]
---

Used in the novel for Winona ("The girl is a quadroon"), for her mother (a "handsome well-educated mulattress"), and for Allen Pinks ("a young mulatto"). The comic keeps the words in the mouths that say them and does not use them in captions.
