---
term: Injun-nigger
gloss: Maybee's slur-compound for Winona and Judah, raised among Indians and Negroes; a clause in his own dialect that the novel then undercuts.
aliases: [Injin-niggers, niggers and Injuns]
tags: [period-term, slur, race]
---

Maybee uses it to Warren in the hotel ("that Injun-nigger! You can't tame him down") while arranging their rescue. His hard words and his deeds disagree: "For all his hard words, Ebenezer Maybee was a humane man". Kept as the novel has it.
