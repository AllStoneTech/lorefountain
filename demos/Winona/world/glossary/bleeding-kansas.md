---
term: Bleeding Kansas
gloss: The 1850s violence over whether Kansas Territory would be slave or free; Maybee's phrase for where the Northern settlers go to "fight it out".
aliases: [the Kansas trouble, Kansas-Nebraska]
tags: [period-term, politics, kansas]
---

The novel's backdrop: Missourians who "took the ballot out of the hands of our citizens" by pouring into Kansas to vote; the sack of Osawatamie; the Colonel's 1854 St. Joseph speech. Maybee says he came because "John Brown's calling for volunteers an' I'm boun' to be in the fight."
