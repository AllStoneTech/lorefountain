---
term: Nigger-thief
gloss: The period slur-compound for a person who helps an enslaved person escape, used by the mob and the court in the novel; a hanging offense in Missouri.
aliases: [slave-stealer]
tags: [period-term, slur, violence]
---

Gideon Holmes calls Warren "a abolitionist an' a nigger thief, two crimes we never overlooks"; Thomson says that in the South such actions "is accountable with murder and becomes a hangin' affair". The judge's sentence is for "aiding slaves to run away". Kept in the comic as the novel has it.
