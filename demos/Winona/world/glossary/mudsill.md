---
term: Mudsill
gloss: The Colonel's period insult for a Northern laborer or abolitionist ("Northern mudsill of an abolitionist").
aliases: [greasy Northern mechanic, psalm singing abolitionist]
tags: [period-term, politics, insult]
---

Used by Colonel Titus when he rebukes Thomson at the cross-roads for turning a British subject into a martyr: "put us in the power of every Northern mudsill of an abolitionist".
