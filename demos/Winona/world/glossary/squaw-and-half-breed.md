---
term: Half-breed
gloss: The period word the narrator and Maybee use for Nokomis, and "squaw" for Seneca women and for Brown's pet name for Winona ("pretty squaw").
aliases: [squaw, pale-face, wigwam]
tags: [period-term, race, indian]
---

Used throughout the first chapters by the narrator ("an old woman, a half-breed, for his housekeeper") and by white characters about Indian women. Brown's "pretty squaw" for Winona is affectionate, and meant that way.
