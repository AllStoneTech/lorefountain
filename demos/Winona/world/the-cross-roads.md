The place outside the Missouri landing where the mob stakes Warren to the foot of a sign-post and heaps fuel around him. The pile is built no higher than his knees "for a slow fire steadily increased would prolong the enjoyment". It is lit by Thomson and put out by Colonel Titus and his constables at dawn.
