The lady the Carlingford brothers quarrelled over. Mr. Pendleton says she and Captain Henry had settled it between them, but her father favored the heir, Lord George, and she later married a nephew of the old lord. Thomson's confession names that nephew as Colonel Titus and says she loved Henry Carlingford "until death". She appears only in other people's accounts.
