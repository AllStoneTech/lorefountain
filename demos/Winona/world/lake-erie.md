The border country of the first and last issues: Buffalo (an anti-slavery stronghold and "the last and most convenient station of the underground railroad"), the lake, the Canadian shore eight miles across, Grand Island, and the constant boom of Niagara. The novel sets it in "the early 50's", among Seneca who gave up the Buffalo Creek reservation in 1842.
