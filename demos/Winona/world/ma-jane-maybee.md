Maybee's wife, who runs the Grand Island Hotel while he is at war and holds a long tin box that White Eagle gave her husband a month before his death. She is mentioned from the first chapter ("My wife's plum crazy to git that young kidabid") and appears only on the last page, folding Winona in her arms.
