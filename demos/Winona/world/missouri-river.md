The border country of Issues 2 to 6: Kansas City and its slave prison and wharf, Weston on the Missouri shore, the ferries that cross into Kansas Territory, the cross-roads and jail and court house on the Missouri side, and the hills near Lawrence on the Kansas side where John Brown's camp stands. The novel dates it to the spring and summer of 1856 and the "troublous times in Kansas", with the sack of Osawatamie behind it.
