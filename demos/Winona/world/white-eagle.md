A white man who lives among the Seneca, saved the chief Red Eagle's life during a cholera outbreak, and since 1842 has kept a cottage on a small island near Grand Island. He brought a fugitive mulattress there from the Underground Railroad, married her in Canada under English law, adopted her small foster child as Judah, and raised Winona after the wife's death. He never told anyone who he had been. He is shot in the back one stormy night in July; only Thomson's confession and a tin box of papers reveal that he was Captain Henry Carlingford, convicted in England of a murder he did not commit.
