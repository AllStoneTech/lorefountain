Ebenezer Maybee's large rambling wooden hotel, "standing back from the road" outside Buffalo, with a piazza all along the front, a bar-room that also serves as office and smoking room, and a long corridor to the rooms above. It is where Warren shelters from the first storm, where the children come at midnight, and where, two years on, the fugitives ride in to a royal welcome.
