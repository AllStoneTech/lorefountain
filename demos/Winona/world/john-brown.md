The historical John Brown as the novel draws him: pastor, tactician and guide to the camp of fugitives in the hills near Lawrence. He is gentle with Winona, stays Judah's hand in the jail, shows Warren his maps and his plan for a slave insurrection, and orders the last prisoners shot after the battle. The novel closes his part by saying that he was "next heard of in the Virginia insurrection which ended so fatally for the intrepid leader".
