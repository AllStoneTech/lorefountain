The mystery under the adventure: who was White Eagle, who killed him, who killed Young Lord George, and who is the heir. Pendleton states the case in Issue 1; the Colonel and Thomson both nearly say it aloud in Issue 3; Thomson solves it in Issue 8. Issues 1 and 2 plant the fair clues (the hunters who join the search and stand at the funeral, the missing papers, "if he lives, Captain Henry is yet amenable to the law").
