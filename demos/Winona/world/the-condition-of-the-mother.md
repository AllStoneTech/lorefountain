The legal trick that takes the children: the traders from Missouri put in a claim "under the new act for the rendition of fugitive slaves jes' passed by Congress" and, Maybee says, "The child follows the condition of the mother. That's the law." Warren protests that both were born free. They are gone before anyone can send them across to Canada. The novel gives no year for the act; see *dating-the-erie-chapters*.
