A young English lawyer sent to America by the firm of Mr. Pendleton to find the missing heir of Carlingford. He comes upon the murder of White Eagle by accident, promises to take Winona and Judah to England, and then spends the novel failing to keep that promise until Thomson's deathbed confession solves his case for him. He is shot, nearly burned at the stake, tried and sentenced to hang in Missouri, and rescued by the people he came to rescue.
