The island girl who is claimed as a slave at fourteen, educated to raise her price, rescued off the steamer *Crescent*, and finally revealed as the daughter and heir of Captain Henry Carlingford. She is shy and stubborn as a child ("a little whirlwind"), steady under captivity, and in the end the one who walks into a Missouri jail dressed as a boy to get Warren out. The novel keeps her innocence and her self-command in the foreground; the comic gives her the plan and the nerve.
