Judah, scout and aide to Brown, finds Winona by the rocks and tells her he is going away as soon as she is safe in Canada; she tells him she has no love to give. "The white man gets it all--all!" A month passes. Steward's wife and children arrive with news: the parson is shot and the young Englishman is in jail. Brown plans a rescue; Maybee will find the jail; Winona prays "Help me to help save him!"
