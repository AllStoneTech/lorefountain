Winona leaves the cave to look for news, hears the scream and sees a body fall into the Possawatomie at her feet: Thomson, alive. Judah arrives and raises his gun; Winona steps in front of it; Warren says "it is enough". The prisoners are shot at sunrise after Warren has begged most of them off; Maybee, wounded, hears the volley, and Thomson, conscious and paralyzed, asks for the Parson and Winona.
