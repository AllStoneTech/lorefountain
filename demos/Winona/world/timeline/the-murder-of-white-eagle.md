White Eagle goes out after supper to look after the canoes and is shot in the back. Judah and Winona cross to the mainland in a storm to fetch Maybee, who brings Warren; they reach the island after midnight. Nokomis says it is too late. Warren finds the bullet hole and says, "This is no accident. It is murder!" Thomson and Titus, in the novel, are the two "strangers" who then help search.
