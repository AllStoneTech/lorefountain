On the Kansas City wharf Warren meets Maybee, now a John Brown man in buckskin. Maybee tells him what Thomson's Rangers have done in Kansas, and they plan the rescue on a pile of lumber where no one can listen: Judah is to get Thomson drunk; Warren is to get off at Weston; Maybee will ride ahead to Parson Steward.
