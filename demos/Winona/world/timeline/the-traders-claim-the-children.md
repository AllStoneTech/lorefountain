The day after Warren leaves, two men who have been "stoppin' roun' hyar fer a month" put in a claim for both children as the property of Missouri slave traders under the new fugitive-slave act, and carry them off before anyone can send them to Canada. Warren returns a month later to hear it from Maybee: "Down on a Missouri plantation, held as a slave!"
