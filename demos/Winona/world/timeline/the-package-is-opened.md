The fugitives ride into Grand Island under an improvised ambulance and a mock divorce threat. Ma' Jane brings out the tin box: marriage and birth certificates, Henry's papers, jeweled portraits and a crested ring. Warren reads them aloud; a Congressman says the "system" is a laughing-stock. Warren tells Judah he will go to England with them.
