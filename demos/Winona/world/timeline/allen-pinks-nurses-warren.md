Warren lies in delirium in a cell above the slave lock-up. A young mulatto prisoner, Allen Pinks, nurses him, quiets the men below through the stove-hole, and kisses his hair when he thinks him asleep. When Warren wakes, Allen tells him that friends are working for him, then that he himself is to be sold, and leaves that night "at the well" and does not return.
