Three hundred Rangers surround the old farmhouse where Judah holds out. Brown rides out with about a hundred men, meets Reynolds's riders (and Steward alive among them), and charges. Warren fights Holmes hand to hand. The Rangers break and run; Judah pursues Colonel Titus across the plain and kills him.
