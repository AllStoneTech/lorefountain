On the road back, Steward's horse stops at a clink of metal. A shot hits Warren's shoulder; Steward cries "Blow ye the trumpet blow! Slay and spare not!" and is shot down. Thomson, Holmes and the Rangers strip Warren of his money, boots and revolvers, offer him a "square deal" for the way to Brown's camp, and get "I can't tell you." They bind him to the parson's horse.
