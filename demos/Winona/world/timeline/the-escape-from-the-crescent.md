Thomson and a cabin of young Virginians drink with a dead man's coffin in the middle of the room until they drop; the steamer sticks on a sandbar half-way to Weston. Warren slips a note through his port-hole to Judah. At a thunderclap Judah lowers a small boat, Warren lowers Winona, and then climbs down to join them.
