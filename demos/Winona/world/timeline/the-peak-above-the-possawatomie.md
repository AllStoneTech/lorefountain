Warren recovers, hears Brown's plan for a slave insurrection, and learns the Rangers are coming. On the peak Winona says she will never marry out of her father's class and will be a nun; Warren says she may marry a duke. A bugle sounds; she nearly falls; he catches her, and they kiss for "one dazzling instant" before the alarm.
