While Winona and Judah are hunting for pigeons, a shot drops a young eagle into the lake and two white hunters land. The children feed them. Thomson says "Likely nigger"; his leader says "Worth five hundred, sure" and, "By George! Thomson, you don't suppose we've struck it at last!" A horn blast from the woods sends the children running. The leader is Colonel Titus; the novel names him only later.
