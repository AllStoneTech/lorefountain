Mr. Pendleton calls Warren into his private office and sends him to find the heir of Carlingford, promising a full partnership if he succeeds. The old lord is dying and thinks Captain Henry may have married in America; his nephew, whom he dislikes, lives in the southern United States. Warren is to keep the mission secret.
