Before they sail, the three go back to the island, climb the ridge, find the Indian-pipes and sit under the pine at White Eagle's grave. The novel closes on what follows: Judah never returns to America, enters the Queen's service, is knighted and marries well; Winona writes to Maybee from England; and Aunt Vinnie sings "Dis is de year of Jubilee."
