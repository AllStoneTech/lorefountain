Thomson climbs the mountain and drops to a ledge above the Possawatomie, and Judah is waiting. He denies him "prisoner of war" because Thomson would never have given it to him, refuses his bribe, and tells him to jump on a count of three. Thomson jumps. Brown and Steward have been watching and cheer. Steward explains his own eight weeks' absence.
