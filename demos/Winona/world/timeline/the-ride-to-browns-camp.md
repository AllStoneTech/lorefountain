They ride at dawn through the woods, Winona between Warren and Judah. Winona dreams "the first enchanted dream of youth"; Judah sees it and hardens. At twilight they reach Brown's camp in the hills near Lawrence, where Winona is taken in by the women. Next morning Warren says, "I shall return in a few weeks at longest" and kisses her hand; Steward and he ride back.
