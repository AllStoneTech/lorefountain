A visitor claiming to come from the British consul slips Warren a note: "Be ready at midnight." Thomson looks in to say saltpetre won't save him. At midnight Maybee and Brown arrive with "a notorious horse thief" and Allen Pinks; the jailer recognizes the boy. Thomson, pistol out, says "if it ain't Winona!" Judah takes him from behind, nearly kills him, and is stopped by Brown. They lock the officials in Warren's cell and carry him out to a wagon in the rain.
