Steward opens to Maybee with a rifle at his chest. The Rangers have sent word they will visit him. He hides Winona in the loft behind a trap-door hung with dried apples and Judah under his own bed behind sea chests, and they talk about the underground railroad and when it is right for a minister to shoot. Two riders come to the door asking for "a nigger man an' gal an' a white man a-pilotin' 'em"; Maybee says "Nary one".
