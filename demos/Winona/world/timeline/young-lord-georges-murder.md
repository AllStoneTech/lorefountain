After a bitter quarrel with his brother Captain Henry over Miss Venton, Young Lord George is found shot in the back through the heart. Thomson confesses on his deathbed that he did it: he was Lord George's valet, and his sister, Miss Venton's maid, had been seduced and abandoned by the lord.
