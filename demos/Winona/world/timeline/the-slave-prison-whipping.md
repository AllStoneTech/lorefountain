Sent to the Kansas City slave prison with a note, Judah is strapped, strung up by his thumbs and whipped, twenty lashes and then ten and then a dozen, on Thomson's order to "teach you a nigger's place". He does not make a sound. Slaves are never whipped on the farm itself because of Lillian's feelings.
