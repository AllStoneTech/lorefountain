White Eagle is buried under the giant pine against which he was found, with Indians from both shores and white curiosity-seekers, including the two strangers, in attendance. Judah vows to find the killer. Warren, who has to go to Virginia on business, leaves money with Maybee and promises to return and take both children to England.
