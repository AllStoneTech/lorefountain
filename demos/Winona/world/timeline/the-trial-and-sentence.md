Warren is paraded through the streets and put on trial in an unfinished court house with three new nooses over his head. He writes a protest as a British subject; the magistrate throws it aside. The Colonel and Thomson testify; the jury says guilty without leaving their seats. He is sentenced to a year in the State prison and then, on Friday the 26th of May, to hang.
