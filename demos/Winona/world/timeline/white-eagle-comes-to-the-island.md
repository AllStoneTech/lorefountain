When the Seneca leave Buffalo Creek in 1842, White Eagle settles on a small island in Lake Erie with a half-deaf housekeeper. He later brings a mulattress who escaped through the Underground Railroad and her small child, marries her in Canada under English law, and adopts the child as Judah. After Winona is born the wife dies.
