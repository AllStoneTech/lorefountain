Winona finds Thomson alive in the river and steps between him and Judah's gun; Warren arrives and stops it. Thomson, his back broken, asks for the Parson, Winona and Judah, and dictates a statement to Warren: White Eagle was Captain Henry Carlingford; Titus killed him; and Thomson himself killed Young Lord George. He dies; a volley outside kills the last of the captured Rangers.
