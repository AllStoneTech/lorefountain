Thomson brings Warren to the Colonel with news of the Carlingford inheritance, and the Colonel and Thomson recognize him from Erie. Over cigars the two men defend slavery as an "institution"; Warren says "A government cannot prosper founded on crushed and helpless humanity". Warren catches sight of Winona and keeps his face still when Judah comes out to take his horse.
