The frame building in the heart of a Missouri city where Warren is held, the unfinished Court House with three new nooses hanging over the dock, and the State prison where he serves out his sentence in a sixteen-foot cell with a grated window at each end. Directly under it is a lock-up for slaves awaiting sale; a stove-hole in the floor lets Warren see and speak to them. The novel does not name the city.
