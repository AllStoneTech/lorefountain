A small wooded island in Lake Erie in the shadow of Grand Island, with a four-room cottage, a beach with a cove, a high ridge looking toward the Canadian shore, and a giant pine over White Eagle's grave. The children keep a canoe in a balsam thicket and a stone fireplace in a cove they call their kitchen. Judah tells the hunters "This island has no name." The novel gives its distance from the mainland as about a mile from Grand Island and, a chapter later, "five miles between the mainland and the island".
