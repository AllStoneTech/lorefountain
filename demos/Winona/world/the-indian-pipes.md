The faint pink-stemmed, gauzy flowers Winona and Judah find on the island in the first chapter. Nokomis says they go away like spirits when touched; Judah says "they turn black as soon as you touch them". The last chapter has them "concealed among the bushes as of old". The comic uses them as its book-end image.
