The Missouri River steamer on which Thomson takes Winona and Judah toward St. Louis to sell them. The slave pen is on deck beside an air-shaft; Warren's stateroom port-hole opens beside it; a coffin sits in the middle of the cabin while young Virginians gamble and sing; the boat strikes a sandbar half-way to Weston in a thunderstorm and the fugitives drop a small boat over the side.
