Thomson's lieutenant. He calls the crowd at the Missouri landing to judgment ("a abolitionist an' a nigger thief, two crimes we never overlooks") and throttles Warren in the hand-to-hand fight at the old farmhouse until Warren clubs him limp with a pistol butt. The novel does not say whether he lives.
