The overseer at Magnolia Farm and the Colonel's closest friend, "a head on him" in the neighbors' phrase. He organizes and drills the Kickapoo Rangers, hunts Winona and Judah for fifteen years, torments Judah into silence, and lights the fire under Warren himself. He is also, as his own confession shows, a former English valet who shot his master's brother in the back to avenge his sister. He goes off a mountain ledge on Judah's count of three and dies in a tent, paralyzed and sane.
