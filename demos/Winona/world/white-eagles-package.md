A long tin box White Eagle gave Maybee about a month before his death "'tell my girl's a re-sponsible age'", which Maybee gave to his wife. It holds three legal documents (the top one a certificate of marriage and birth), a record of Henry's life in America, jeweled family portraits including one of Captain Henry as a young man, and a ring bearing the family crest. Warren, who is a lawyer, finds "the chain of evidence was complete".
