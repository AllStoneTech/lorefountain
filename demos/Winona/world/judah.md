White Eagle's adopted son, the child of a fugitive mother who died on the road to Canada. He paddles, shoots and rides like a Seneca, speaks "like a senator", and is made assistant overseer and horse trainer at Magnolia Farm. He is whipped by his thumbs for it, learns to say nothing, and swears to kill Thomson. In the novel's last page he is knighted in England and marries into a good family.
