The novel uses the name Lord George for two men. Mr. Pendleton says that "young Lord George" was found dead, shot in the back, after quarrelling with his brother Captain Henry over Miss Venton; the same speech then calls "Lord George" an old man of eighty-five who wants Henry's children to inherit. Chapter VI has Warren call Henry "Lord George's younger son" and tell Titus "Your uncle died six months ago"; the last chapter has Winona's grandfather "old Lord George" alive to sanction the marriage. See the note *which-lord-george*. The comic calls the murdered brother YOUNG LORD GEORGE on the page and the dying baronet OLD LORD GEORGE.
