Belton's mother, Hannah Lizabeth Piedmont, a washerwoman in [[Winchester]] and mother of five: James Henry (15), Amanda Ann (13), Eliza Jane (11), Belton (8), Celestine (5). The family lives in a one-room rock house. When the teacher's hatred makes her consider withdrawing Belton, her parson advises her to keep him in school. She comes to Belton's Stowe graduation, and Belton boards with her again at Winchester after Antoinette's child is born.
