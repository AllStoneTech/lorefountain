An old town in the northwest corner of Virginia, where Belton and Bernard grow up in the same colored school and where Belton goes to his mother's house after his son is born white. It contains the one-room school (a condemned Baptist church), Hannah's rock cottage, and the commencement stage with a cistern under it.
