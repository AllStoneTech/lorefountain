Secretary of State of the Imperium from the day of its organization, "a warm personal friend of both Bernard and Belton" (Griggs's preface), and the author of the novel's papers. He is one of the five who fire the fatal shots at Belton, decides to prove traitor and reveal the Imperium's existence "that it might be broken up or watched," and expects to be shot as a traitor; his Dying Declaration prefaces the book. The novel does not say how he reveals it.
