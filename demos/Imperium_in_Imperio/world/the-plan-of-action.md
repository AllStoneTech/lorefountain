Bernard's eight-point scheme on a foolscap sheet: reconsider making the Imperium known and avoid all mention of emigration to Texas; secretly buy Texas land contiguous to states and territories and build commonplace huts over rapid-fire disappearing guns in fortifications dug beneath them, paid for by Imperium bonds; encourage Negroes to enter the U.S. Navy; negotiate secretly with the foreign enemies of the United States; get a Congressional appropriation for a fair at Galveston, inviting the Governor; seize Austin while he is away and hoist the Imperium's flag; wreck the U.S. navy in a night if need be; demand the surrender of Texas and Louisiana, keeping Texas and ceding Louisiana to the foreign allies. Belton says "It is treason," votes no, and resigns, which costs him his life.
