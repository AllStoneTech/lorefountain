The teacher of the colored school at [[Winchester]], a Confederate colonel who shot his own commander in the back, deserted, and has lived under that secret since. He gives flowers and dinners to [[Bernard Belgrave]], torments [[Belton Piedmont]], and is dropped into the cistern at the school's commencement. Years later he is in [[Norfolk]] acting for Bernard's father, passing as a radical Democrat, and wins Bernard's House seat by buying a tabulated statement of the fraud for five thousand dollars. He disappears from the novel's story after that. He leaves Winchester "for parts unknown" the night of the cistern, having served twelve years.
