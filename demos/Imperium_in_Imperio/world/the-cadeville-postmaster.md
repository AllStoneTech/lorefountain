The president of the [[Nigger Rulers]] and the town's postmaster. He has kept a good eye on [[Belton Piedmont]] and, on [[Dr. Zackland]]'s word that Belton took a seat downstairs in a white church, orders him hanged. He fires the pistol at the base of Belton's skull himself, and he is the foreman of the jury that convicts Belton of Zackland's death.
