A mansion of splendid appearance in one of the most fashionable portions of Washington; the bearded guide has a key to which all the doors respond, and the Senator's room is a large room at the rear on the second floor with two windows, east and north. It is the setting of the scene where [[Bernard Belgrave]] learns who his father is and what the family has spent a lifetime hiding.
