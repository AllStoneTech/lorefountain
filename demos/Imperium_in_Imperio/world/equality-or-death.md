The password of the secret student society Belton founds at [[Stowe University]], printed on white boards the students raise in unison against their faculty. It is the first time he sees the power of organization, and it is Belton's first taste of rebellion against the whites, and the novel says that in after life he is "the moving, controlling, guiding spirit" of one on a far larger scale.
