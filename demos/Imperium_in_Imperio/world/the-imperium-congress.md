The legislature of [[The Imperium in Imperio]]: one chamber with one member for every fifty thousand citizens, elected by majority and recallable at any time. Whenever a bill affecting the race is introduced in the U.S. Congress it is also introduced and debated here. The novel says one member for every fifty thousand citizens, which at 7,250,000 would be 145.
