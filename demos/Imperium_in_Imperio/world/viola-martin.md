The Norfolk beauty [[Bernard Belgrave]] falls for at first sight. She loves him, but a book on racial extinction has convinced her that mixed-race marriages doom the Black race, and she has solemnly pledged God never to marry a mulatto man. On the night after she tells Bernard she will never be his wife she turns on the gas in her room, rather than break the vow, leaving [[Viola's Letter]] to explain why.
