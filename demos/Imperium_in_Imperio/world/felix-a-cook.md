A colored man of ability, culture and refinement, appointed postmaster of [[Lake City]], South Carolina, by President McKinley. A white mob sets fire to his house while the occupants sleep and riddles him with bullets as he tries to escape; his wife and children are wounded. He was a member of the Imperium; his heart, raked from the embers, lies in a golden casket in his draped seat when the Congress meets. Mrs. Cook rises and tells her story to the Congress.
