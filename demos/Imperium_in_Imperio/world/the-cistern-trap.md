A rock-walled cistern Belton secretly digs from the wood-shed to under the school-room platform, directly under the spot where the teacher always stands. Its cut board is held up by a prop with a weight attached; a rope runs from the weight to a hole under Belton's seat. When he pulls it, the teacher drops into water a little more than waist deep.
