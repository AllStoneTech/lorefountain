After Belton finds a white girl's hymn in a Monroe church, the Nigger Rulers hang him and the postmaster shoots him at the base of the skull. Dr. Zackland has the body carried to his dissecting table; Belton, only wounded, kills the doctor with his own knife, flees to the Governor at Baton Rouge, and is sent back under military escort to be tried.
