The male students, led by Belton, and through one student's sister the young women, sign a complaint demanding the colored teacher be allowed to eat with the white teachers, and it is laid on the president's desk before devotions. When the president rings the gong to dismiss them, nobody moves, and every raised hand holds a sign reading "Equality or Death." The faculty capitulates.
