At Waco, Belton says he has unearthed a conspiracy and means to expose it, and when Bernard refuses to join him, has six men aim rifles at him (with blank cartridges) and then a gallows rope at him. Bernard calls him an infernal scoundrel and spits in his face. The rifles fire blanks; the platform's trapdoor drops him, with a parachute, into the Imperium's congressional chamber.
