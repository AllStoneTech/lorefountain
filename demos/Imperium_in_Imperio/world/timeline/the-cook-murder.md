A white mob sets fire to Postmaster Cook's house at night in Lake City, South Carolina, and riddles him with bullets as the family tries to escape; his wife and children are wounded. His heart is raked from the burning embers the next morning and placed in a golden casket in his draped seat in the Imperium's Congress.
