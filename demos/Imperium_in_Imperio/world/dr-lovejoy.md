The president of [[Stowe University]], a venerable white minister from the North. He receives the students' paper during devotions, rings the gong three times, and gives way; he preaches the baccalaureate sermon "The Kingdom of God is within us," and he talks Belton out of vengeance after the socks. The novel names him only in Chapter VII. Cadeville later asks him to recommend a president.
