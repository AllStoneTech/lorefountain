The film's organizing question: loyal to whom? Belton will not take part in what he calls treason against the United States even at the cost of his life; Bernard will not betray his people even to save his best friend; Berl Trout betrays his own government "in the name of humanity." In the novel Trout calls himself both traitor and patriot.
