A made-to-order wig and a set of women's dresses [[Belton Piedmont]] buys in New York. Dressed in them he is hired as a nurse in the family of a leading white man in [[Richmond]] so he can listen at keyholes to what white people say about the Negro. The disguise ends when a number of young men resort to kidnapping and overpowering him.
