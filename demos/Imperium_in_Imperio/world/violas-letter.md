The farewell letter [[Viola Martin]] leaves for [[Bernard Belgrave]]. It explains the vow that kept her from marrying him, signs itself "Viola Belgrave," and asks him to study the question of race mixing and, if she is right, to separate the races morally or physically. It is the origin of Bernard's vow that "the races... must separate."
