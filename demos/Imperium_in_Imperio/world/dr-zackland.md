[[Cadeville]]'s doctor, who has agreed with the postmaster that he will have [[Belton Piedmont]]'s body to dissect in exchange for a keg of whiskey. He witnesses Belton find a white girl's hymn in a Monroe church and carries the news to the postmaster. After the mob hangs Belton, he has the body brought to his dissecting table; Belton, who has only been feigning death, stabs him through the throat with the doctor's own knife. The novel spells his name "Zakeland" once, in the postmaster's mouth, and "Zackland" everywhere else.
