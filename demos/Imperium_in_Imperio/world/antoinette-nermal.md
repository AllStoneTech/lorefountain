A teacher at the Richmond colored school where [[Belton Piedmont]] gets his first job. They court through the kissing game "In the well" and marry about a year after Belton becomes a stamping clerk. When a white baby is born to them, Belton flees, and her church tries and excludes her on a charge of adultery; she does not attend the trial or speak in her defense. As the child darkens, her church rescinds its action and society comes rushing to her. She prays hourly for Belton's return.
