The belief, taken from [[Viola Martin]]'s reading of a book called *White Supremacy and Negro Subordination*, that race mixing is slowly destroying the Black race and that the answer is moral or physical separation. Bernard inherits it as a grief-driven commission and turns it into a political program. The film treats it as Viola's passionate conviction and Bernard's blind spot, and does not endorse it. Viola's letter gives the book's claims: that the fourth generation of children of intermarrying mulattoes are invariably sterile or lacking in vital force.
