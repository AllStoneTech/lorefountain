The name the novel gives (Chapter XII) to the white secret society of northern Louisiana around [[Cadeville]]. The postmaster is its president and the teacher of the white public school is the captain of its army. It enforces the color line by terror: it cut a white man who distributed republican ballots into four pieces and threw him into the Ouachita River, and it hunted down the brothers Samuel and John Bowser after they voted. It takes [[Belton Piedmont]] into the woods to hang him. The name contains a racial slur; the screenplay keeps it because the novel does (see the content note in `imports/source-material.md`).
