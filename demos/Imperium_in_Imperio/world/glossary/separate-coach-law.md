---
term: Separate Coach Law
gloss: A state law requiring separate railroad coaches for colored and white passengers.
aliases: [Jim Crow car, first-class coach rule]
tags: [period-term, segregation]
---

The novel says that when Belton travels to Louisiana the law "had not been enacted by any of the Southern States," but in Louisiana the whites had "an unwritten but inexorable law" that no Negro ride in a first-class coach. White passengers throw [[Belton Piedmont]] off the moving train on his way to [[Cadeville]]. Belton later cites the Supreme Court's decision holding Separate Coach Laws constitutional.
