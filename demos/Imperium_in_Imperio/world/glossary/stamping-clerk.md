---
term: Stamping clerk
gloss: A post office clerk who cancels outgoing mail — a routine clerical job, here the job Belton is given instead of the postmastership his party work had earned.
aliases: [stamping clerkship]
tags: [period-term, patronage]
---

The novel says Belton, measured by influence and real ability, "was entitled to the best place in the district in the gift of the government" but "the color of his skin was against him." He is paid sixty dollars a month and is dismissed when he wins a congressional nomination for a candidate the postmaster did not want.
