---
term: Mob law
gloss: The period term for lynching and mob violence replacing legal process; in the novel, "lynch-law."
aliases: [lynch law, mob violence]
tags: [period-term, violence]
---

[[Belton Piedmont]]'s speech in the Imperium's Congress names mob law, in the novel's phrase "of all the curses that have befallen the South, this is the greatest,", and the Congress hears it with Postmaster Cook's heart in a golden casket nearby.
