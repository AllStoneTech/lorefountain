The old Confederate capital, where Belton teaches in a colored school (through Mr. King's influence), meets and courts Antoinette, starts a weekly journal and printing office, is dismissed from the school, becomes a stamping clerk at the Post Office and is dismissed again, marries Antoinette, and disguises himself as a nurse to learn what white people think of the Negro.
