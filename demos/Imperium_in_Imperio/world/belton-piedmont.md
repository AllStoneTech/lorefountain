The poorest boy in the colored school at [[Winchester]] and the best orator in it. Belton is trained by a teacher who hates him into a student who cannot be beaten, goes to [[Stowe University]] on an editor's check, and is a member of the Imperium for years (chairman of its bureau of education at Stowe, at work for it in Cadeville), and after his Cadeville trial is elected to its Congress and soon chosen Speaker. He believes the United States owes Black Americans the rights it promised, and that the right way to collect is by law, vote, and persuasion rather than by war. When the Imperium chooses otherwise, he refuses to follow it.
