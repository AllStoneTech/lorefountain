A U.S. senator whose name, state and committee the novel withholds, and who married [[Fairfax Belgrave]] in secret in Canada. He pays [[Tiberius Gracchus Leonard]] two thousand dollars a year to teach Bernard, tells Bernard the truth in a Washington mansion, and later secretly votes for him in the House (in the novel, "Bernard's father had redeemed his promise of secret support"). His war-years bargain with Leonard, secret for secret, is why he uses him: "you see that he is a cowardly villain; but we sometimes have to use such."
