[[Viola Martin]]'s mother. She sits sewing while Viola writes; she smells the gas on the stairs and falls screaming at Viola's door; Viola's letter asks her to take "poor Bernard for your son." Viola's father is a clerk in the post-office and was a colonel in the Civil War.
