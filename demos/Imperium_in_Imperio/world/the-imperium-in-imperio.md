A government of 7,250,000 people (Belton also says "over seven million five hundred thousand" in the same speech) inside the United States: a one-chamber Congress with one member for every fifty thousand citizens, elected by majority vote for an indefinite term and recallable at any time; branch legislatures in each state; a judiciary; an army of every able-bodied citizen; a treasury of $500,000,000 and citizens' property of about $350,000,000, $850,000,000 in all. It grew from a secret society founded in the early days of the Republic by a wealthy Negro scientist, whose millions bought Southern land at twenty-five cents an acre; after the war the society persuaded every existing Negro secret order to add one more, identical degree, and that degree is the government. Its Secretary of State is Berl Trout.
