An enclosure of about twenty acres, five miles outside [[Waco]], with a high stone wall: a four-story building about two hundred feet long and one hundred and eighty wide, with a round tower and an elevator, and a small white cottage nearby. The fourth floor is one huge room with a table, two chairs and a ten-foot platform that looks like a gallows and is a trap door; below, a parachute lets a man land in the large first-floor room with 145 desks in a semicircle, the Imperium's Congress. It is "in reality the Capitol of our Government." The President's mansion and the execution knoll are on the grounds.
