The senator's legitimate son, raised in luxury by a mother who never explains herself, Harvard's valedictorian, congressman from Virginia's Second District (after a contested election), and the most noted negro of his day. After [[Viola Martin]]'s death he vows that the races must separate, takes the presidency of [[The Imperium in Imperio]] after three months of examination, calls it to war with his message, and, when Belton turns the Congress, writes the Plan of Action to seize Texas. He commands the firing squad that shoots Belton and vows at the grave to give the Anglo-Saxons' flesh to the buzzards.
