A wealthy, cultured woman, daughter of a governor's brother and a servant in his father's household, who arrives in [[Winchester]] the day before her son starts school and lives in strict isolation. She is the senator's wife in the eyes of the law and his mistress in the eyes of everyone else, and she has accepted the shame to protect his career. She sends a note by a bearded stranger that sends Bernard to [[The Senator's Mansion]], where his father tells him the truth. She reared Bernard a devoted Catholic, with a library and unlimited money.
